Major live systems

  • First magick and spell-list iterations
  • Initial psionics and Berserk implementations
  • Identify Armor, Weapon, Gems, and Magick
  • Pick Pocket, Defend, Guard, maps, grouping, and social improvements

Still Being Built and Tested

Phase 1 foundation work continues

  • Class abilities: live spell and skill sets continue to be balanced and corrected as testing restores missing data.
  • Racial abilities: races are selectable, but racial skill implementation is not yet complete across the roster.
  • Content depth: trainers, creatures, loot, quests, and world content continue to expand around the rebuilt systems.
  • Documentation: some archive pages describe the classic game rather than current beta behavior; live-status labels and updated guides are being added.
  • Secure connection: port 9555 responds, but its current certificate can cause warnings. Port 8555 remains the dependable connection.

Roadmap Context

We are in Phase 1, around Month 6

Phase 1 spans Months 0–12 and focuses on the core systems foundation. Later phases use six-month planning windows. These are planning ranges, not guaranteed completion dates.
