Welcome

Abandon all preconceived notions, ye who enter here. This may look like someplace you have been, but Dragon's Gate Reborn is unique unto itself and holds many surprises for you. Please do not assume anything based on experiences you have had elsewhere or elsewhen. We encourage you to read the manual, which contains many hints and suggestions on surviving and excelling in this new world of Dragon's Gate Reborn.

Common sense will always be your best companion. - DGR Staff

Introduction

An interactive, real time, fantasy role-playing game based on ideas similar to some fantasy stories you may have enjoyed. Each player creates a character and assumes the identity of that character while in Dragon's Gate Reborn. Role-playing allows interaction between player characters and non-player characters in the form of computer controlled monsters, merchants, and more.

You can immerse yourself in the game world while participating in game master or player-run scenarios, going adventuring, completing quests, competing in gladiator games and making new friends.

Start here

Your character can be comprised of any one of several classes and races and can be individualized in many ways.

Life in Dragon's Gate Reborn continues 24 hours a day, and events continue to occur without requiring your direct intervention.

Welcome to Dragon's Gate Reborn. We hope you enjoy the time you spend here.
