Create your account at the game login

No website form, invitation, or email address is required.

Verified against the live game on August 9, 2026. The exact wording may change as the beta improves.

Account setup

  1. Connect to game.dragonsgatereborn.com on port 8555.
  2. Choose 2. Create new account.
  3. Enter an account name. It is formatted as one capitalized word and must be different from your character name.
  4. Confirm the account name, then enter and confirm a password. The game does not require an email address.
  5. At the account menu, choose C. Create new character.

Keep the account name and password private. Staff and community helpers do not need your password to assist you.

Character setup

  1. Name: choose and confirm a first and last name.
  2. Race: select from the current roster. Type help <race> for background information; use detail to show class and alignment compatibility.
  3. Identity: choose alignment, sex, age, weight, height, eyes, hair, skin, and mood. Physical ranges vary by race.
  4. Class: choose a calling allowed for your race. Cleric Heart, Sword, and Hand appear as separate selections. Dracos do not choose a class.
  5. Deity: choose from the options allowed by your alignment and class. None may be available.
  6. Characteristics: accept or reroll your generated stats. Stars identify rolls favorable to your selected class.
  7. Weapon school: begin with Edge, Blunt, or Thrust Weapons. Other weapon schools can be learned in the game.
  8. Final choice: decide whether to enlist in the Mercenary Guild tour, then enter the world.

Important Choice

Mercenary Guild tour and permanent death

The optional Mercenary Guild tour is presented as a sharper training path with the risk of real, permanent character death. Read both confirmation screens carefully. If you are unsure, choose No; ordinary beta play does not require joining the tour.

Current creation roster

There are currently 21 race choices and 10 class selections. Muatana-al are restricted to one per account. Wyverns are documented in the archive but are not currently selectable.
