How This Works

Real observations, not estimates

  • Five-minute monitoring: the status service checks the public game menu every five minutes and records whether the world responded.
  • Adventurer activity: averages and peaks use the adventurer count reported by the game. Staff roles are not included in those totals.
  • Privacy: historical records contain only reachability, time, and aggregate player counts. Character names are shown live but are not stored in activity history.
  • Uptime meaning: uptime represents successful checks from the monitoring service. A network interruption between the monitor and game can also appear as a missed signal.
  • New tracker: monitoring began in August 2026. The 24-hour and seven-day summaries will become more representative as real samples accumulate.
