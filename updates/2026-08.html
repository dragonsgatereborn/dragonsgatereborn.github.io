August 2026

Pick Pocket refinements, social and command improvements, and the public Muatana-al release.

August 2, 2026 at 14:00 - dayrinni

Pick Pocket Improvements and Muatana-al Release

Good day! Here is the update to the update. Enjoy reading the update notes for this update.

System and command updates:

  • Pick Pocket now checks the applicable safe-room flags.
  • NPCs now have a short-term “staring” memory of recent pick attempts.
  • Pick Pocket equations were updated using newly collected data: the fumble chance was reduced substantially, the empty-purse check now occurs only after a successful pick, and the base success rate was adjusted to more closely match the data.
  • A successful pick starts the combat timer to prevent immediate logout. A Pick Pocket-specific timer may be considered if testing shows that the combat timer does not work well.
  • Players can now look at NPCs to see their inventory, including how much silver they are carrying.
  • The damaged-item warning was removed, and item repair is no longer unlimited.
  • Socials, Emotes, and Smotes received improved punctuation and target-possessive handling. For example, emote 's eyes glow red! and emote looks at @target's face. now format with the character and target names.
  • The room inspect message was removed.
  • Defend/Guard now has a 100% rate and performs a range check.

Muatana-al public release:

  • A publicly available version of the Muatana-al race was released.
  • Players are limited to one Muatana-al per account at any given time.
  • Muatana-al currently have attributes similar to Humans and, like the other races at this stage of development, do not yet have racial skills.
  • After class work is completed, racial skills will begin to be added for all races. Muatana-al are planned to receive balanced versions of their racial abilities while remaining susceptible to sunlight damage and permanent death.

The one-per-account restriction required changes to account code. Several rounds of testing were completed because of the critical nature of account data. Please report any account-related issues immediately.

Changed characters are backed up nightly, backups are created automatically during copyovers, and manual backups are routinely made—generally before every update—so two update-time backups are normally available.

Thank you!

DGR Team
