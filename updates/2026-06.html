Training Grounds Guild Bank Enabled

Good day!

Here is the update to the update. Enjoy reading the update notes for this update.

The Training Grounds Guild Bank has been flagged as BANK. This means you can use the BANK command to store coins and the BOX command to store items. These are separate commands.

Type "bank" to enter banking mode.

Type "box view" to see what is in your bank vault.

Type "box store <inventory slot>" to store an item from your inventory.

Type "box remove <bank slot>" to remove an item from the vault.

This is currently themed as ATS, so do not be alarmed! There will be future updates to make it behave and resemble DG.

Please test the above to ensure everything is working correctly!

Thank you!

DGR Team
