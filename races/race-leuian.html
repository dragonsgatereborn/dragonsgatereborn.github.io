Overview

Leuians are fierce warriors, considered some of the most fearsome in the lands. These feline-humanoid beings feature retractable claws, tails, fur, and walk upright. They live by The Precepts, a strict ethical code serving as their law, emphasizing honor, truth, and direct engagement while disdaining deception, theft, and dishonesty.

Abilities & Combat Traits

Strengths:

  • Night vision capability
  • Exceptional speed and agility in combat
  • Retractable claws that sharpen with repeated use
  • Natural warriors with fearsome reputation

Limitations:

  • Cannot wear metal armor
  • Limited aptitude for magic

The Seven Precepts

Leuians follow The Precepts, a strict code of honor that defines their culture and governs their behavior:

  1. Remain a free people - Independence and autonomy are sacred
  2. Deal with honor and truth - Honesty in all dealings
  3. Never spill innocent blood - Protect the innocent
  4. Leuians cannot kill other Leuians - Unity of the race
  5. Honor forbids unjust punishment - Justice must be fair
  6. Cherish the land - Respect for nature and home
  7. Embrace diversity - Accept differences among people

The Precepts are law, culture, and way of life for all honorable Leuians.

Culture & Society

Religion: Most Leuians worship Rinanni, though cultural practices supersede religious observance. The Precepts guide daily life more than religious doctrine.

Tarr'khats - The Outcast

Leuian thieves and followers of Set are called "Tarr'khats" - they are branded as traitors to honorable Leuian society. These outcasts have abandoned the Precepts and are shunned by their people.

Values

  • Honor above all else
  • Direct confrontation over deception
  • Truth in word and deed
  • Protection of the innocent
  • Unity among Leuians
  • Respect for nature and homeland

Role-Play Considerations

  • Honor and truth must guide all your actions
  • The Precepts are not suggestions - they are absolute law
  • Never harm another Leuian - this is the ultimate taboo
  • Deception and thievery are deeply dishonorable
  • Combat prowess is a source of pride and cultural identity
  • Cannot rely on metal armor - adapt combat strategy accordingly
  • Limited magic means focus on physical prowess
  • Tarr'khats are outcasts - choosing this path has serious consequences
