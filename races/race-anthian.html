Overview

Anthians are short humanoids with bird/reptilian-like features. They possess sharp bird-like facial characteristics and distinctive feather-covered wings on their backs, making them renowned for natural flight. Their exceptionally beautiful voices sound almost like the soft chirping of birds.

Physical Characteristics

Eye Colors

Red, Brown, Hazel

Hair Colors

Blonde, Sandy, Yellow

Height

Males: 4'0" - 5'2"
Females: 3'7" - 4'8"

Build

Short humanoids with bird/reptilian features and feathered wings

Abilities & Traits

Strengths:

  • Natural flight capability
  • Night vision
  • Exceptionally beautiful singing voices
  • Sharp bird-like features

Limitations:

  • Lightweight build prevents carrying substantial cargo
  • Small stature limits physical strength

Culture & Society

Anthians are notably reclusive, preferring small communities in natural settings like canyons, bluffs, and crags over urban environments. Their mentality parallels Leuian culture - they exhibit strong pride and fierce dedication to family and community bonds.

Habitat Preferences

Anthians favor natural terrain features:

  • Canyons
  • Bluffs and cliffs
  • Crags and rocky outcroppings
  • Small, isolated communities
  • Avoid large urban centers

Cultural Values

  • Strong pride in heritage and abilities
  • Fierce dedication to family
  • Deep community bonds
  • Reclusive nature - value privacy
  • Love of natural beauty and song

Bards represent the most frequent urban profession among Anthians, as their beautiful voices make them natural performers.

Alignment & Religion

Alignment: May follow any alignment

Primary Deity: Elindale (traditionally favored)

Other Faiths: Any religion may be practiced

Role-Play Considerations

  • Flight provides unique mobility - use it creatively
  • Beautiful voice makes you natural bard or performer
  • Reclusive nature creates interesting urban interactions
  • Strong family/community bonds drive character motivation
  • Limited cargo capacity requires careful inventory management
  • Small size affects combat reach and strength
  • Prefer natural settings over cities
  • Pride in heritage shapes interactions with other races
