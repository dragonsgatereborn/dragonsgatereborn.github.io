Overview

Goblins and Go-blin-als are two different races, not two names for the same people. Their histories and identities are separate, but their current gameplay statistics, professions, abilities, and restrictions are the same.

Both are cunning, resourceful, and commonly underestimated by larger races. They are pack rats and save nearly everything they find, much of which is repugnant to the general population.

Separate History

Goblin Background

Goblins belong to Aradath's greenskin peoples and have a racial identity and history of their own. They should not be treated as a shortened name for Go-blin-als or automatically assigned the Go-blin-al origin story simply because the two races share gameplay mechanics.

The recovered archives currently preserve less confirmed history about Goblins than Go-blin-als. This section can be expanded as additional Goblin records and community knowledge are recovered.

Separate History

Go-blin-al Background

Editor's note: Go-blin-al is pronounced with a long “o,” as in the word “go.”

Go-blin-als are servants to Chaos and are thought by scholars to have been created through the warped experiments of the Chaos Lords. It is rumored they are remnants of a once-powerful race, twisted into their present form by magicks horrifying and unimaginable.

They dwell in caves and tight-knit clans, thriving in darkness and disorder. They delight in trouble, and their small size keeps them weak in extended fights, but they are quick and dangerous in packs.

Shared Gameplay — Applies to Both Races

Appearance

Unless a section specifically discusses racial history, the following appearance, temperament, tendencies, traits, speech guidance, and profession options apply equally to Goblins and Go-blin-als.

Height

About 3'0"

Build

Ugly, bipedal, mottled green skin, usually filthy

Eye Colors

Brown, Red, Black

Hair Colors

Sandy, Brown, Red, Black

Temperament

Nasty, violent, and full of mischief. They are punks and bullies by nature, sadistic when the balance is in their favor, and cowardly when it turns against them.

Tendencies

Goblins and Go-blin-als avoid sunlight, live in clans, and love to harass weaker creatures. Their bravado fades against real threats, but their speed and numbers can be deadly. A large experienced pack is like fighting a small army.

Spitting at each other is a common form of greeting.

Traits

Advantages: Night vision, quickness, agility, and dexterity.

Hindrances: Known troublemakers and fairly weak. Speech and societal barriers with other races.

Abilities: A propensity for getting into mischief and trouble.

Alignment: Chaotic

Professions: Thief. Rare: Fighter, Barbarian, Rune Mage.

Speech

Goblins and Go-blin-als tend to speak with something between a lisp and poorly developed Common. Some may speak clearly, but most follow patterns like these:

Bushwig exclaims, "zkrew dat!"

Condie exclaims, "wut yoo wuntz!"

Condie asks, "gotz mah lolliz?"

Condie says, "juz yoo weit.."

Leroy exclaims, "iz krazy in heeyah!"

Bushwig says, "dat ting almoz kil yuz"

Bushwig says, "diz ting kilin dem"

Role-Play Considerations

  • Lean into mischief, bravado, and clan dynamics
  • Play the cowardly-but-dangerous-in-groups angle
  • Use speech patterns to reinforce race flavor
  • Quickness and agility keep you alive in close fights
  • Sunlight aversion and cave life shape habits
