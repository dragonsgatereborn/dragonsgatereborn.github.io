Overview

Dragons are the most powerful of all the races of Dragon's Gate Reborn. They represent the apex of power within the game's racial hierarchy, growing through distinct life stages from young Draco to the ancient and mighty Imperial dragon.

Unlike other races, dragons operate outside standard game structures - they lack a designated class system and do not worship the mortal pantheon of gods.

Life Stages

Dragons progress through four distinct developmental phases as they grow in power and experience:

Stage 1: Draco

The baseline body plan for dragonkind: small, mostly humanoid, and still discovering the world.

  • No wings
  • Softer features
  • Curious, cautious posture
  • Reads as: young, vulnerable, learning

Stage 2: Drake

A larger Draco with newly developed wings and a clear surge in confidence and power.

  • Same humanoid torso and stance
  • Newly developed wings
  • Increased muscle and confidence
  • Reads as: adolescent, emerging power

Stage 3: Dragon

Fully draconic and regal, with a heavier frame and a distinct reduction in humanoid proportions.

  • Full wings
  • Heavier scale plating
  • Reduced humanoid proportions
  • Reads as: mature, powerful, apex being

Stage 4: Imperial

An ancient, massive presence with a calm, knowing expression and a mythic aura.

  • Larger horns
  • Broader frame
  • Composed, timeless bearing
  • Reads as: mythic, near-divine

Unique Characteristics

  • Most powerful race in Dragon's Gate Reborn
  • Night vision (draconic senses)
  • No class system - dragons are their own class
  • Do not worship mortal gods
  • Progress through four life stages with advancement
  • Abilities and power scale dramatically with each stage
  • Form special bonds with Secians during staging processes

Bonding with Secians

Dragons form critical bonding partnerships with Secians, the tiny winged fairy-like race. These bonds are essential during dragon staging processes, when dragons transition from one life stage to the next.

The relationship between dragons and Secians is one of mutual benefit and deep magical significance in the world of Aradath.

Role-Play Considerations

  • Dragons are the apex predators and most powerful beings in Aradath
  • Your power level changes dramatically as you progress through stages
  • No traditional classes means unique character development path
  • Consider your relationship with other races, particularly Secians
  • Dragons operate outside many social norms that bind other races
  • Long lifespan and growth stages provide unique RP opportunities
