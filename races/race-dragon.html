Overview

Dragons are the most powerful of all the races of Dragon's Gate Reborn. They represent the apex of power within the game's racial hierarchy, growing through distinct life stages from young Draco to the ancient and mighty Imperial dragon.

Unlike most other races, dragons operate outside the standard game structure. They have no designated class system and do not worship the mortal pantheon of gods.

Life Stages

All dragons born since the Great Change begin as dracos and progress through four distinct stages. Early stages can be weaker than the average adventurer; later stages grow dramatically in size, resilience, and magickal power.

Each new stage will eventually unlock additional spells. The exact spell lists will be added as they are finalized through development and testing.

Stage 1: Draco

Roughly human-sized and human-shaped, a draco can easily be mistaken for a human at first glance. This is a dragon's youngest and most vulnerable stage.

  • No wings, scales, or tail
  • No fire breath or combat-capable claws and bite
  • Mostly human features, including hair and skin colors
  • Curious, young, and still finding their place in the world

Stage 2: Drake

A dragon reaches this stage by surviving an intense and painful transition. The resulting drake is more draconic, but remains humanoid and can fly.

  • Humanoid build and stance with scales, wings, and functional claws
  • No tail or fire breath
  • Retains the ability to use weapons, shields, and armor
  • Increased size, strength, natural protection, and magickal prowess
  • Proud and increasingly certain of their role in the community

Stage 3: Dragon

A fully grown adult with four legs, wings, a long tail and neck, and powerful jaws. This is the classic dragon of legend—and often the most dangerous being in the area.

At this stage, dragons become tempting targets for would-be hunters. Their bodies are used in many alchemical and martial pursuits, from potions and draughts to armor and weapons. Their essence is potent enough for use in rituals and other alchemical preparations.

By this stage, most dragons have found their place in the world and their communities. Many begin searching for a lair and spending less time in cities.

  • Thick scales and a fully draconic body prevent the use of weapons, shields, and armor
  • Significant increase in size, strength, and magickal prowess
  • Natural attacks include fire breath and a powerful bite
  • Current skill guidance grants biting to third-stage-and-higher dragons

Stage 4: Imperial

An ancient, fully realized dragon. Imperials are the largest and strongest dragons in Aradath, and the rarest form most people will ever encounter.

An Imperial's essence is at its most potent, making these ancient dragons extraordinarily valuable—and extraordinarily dangerous—targets for alchemists and hunters. Few who pursue one succeed.

  • Stronger, thicker scales and still greater physical power
  • Exceptional magickal prowess and association with ancient draconic magicks
  • Dragon essence reaches its greatest potency
  • Elders of their species—often reclusive and rarely encountered

Bonding with Secians

No one can say with certainty what triggered the first bond. The surviving histories agree, however, that dragons rely on Secian healing to survive the painful staging process safely.

Attempting to stage without a bondmate can mean permanent death or the loss of sanity. Even with a bond, the transformation can endanger both dragon and Secian.

When a young draco chooses a Secian partner, both place their lives in the other's hands. A Secian's empathic nature allows them to heal—and feel—the pain and distress of the dragon's physical transformation.

Staging seals the pair as bondmates for life, and every later stage draws them closer. The bond transcends ordinary friendship or marriage: each becomes part of the other. This history gives dragons and Secians a natural affinity and a deep responsibility to care for one another.

Unique Characteristics

  • Progresses from a vulnerable early form to one of Aradath's most powerful beings
  • Night vision and draconic senses
  • No traditional class system—dragons are their own class
  • Does not worship the mortal pantheon
  • Power, abilities, and physical form change dramatically across four stages
  • Forms a lifelong, roleplay-intensive bond with a Secian through staging

Roleplay Considerations

  • Dragon characters begin as vulnerable dracos; strength and perspective change dramatically after each staging
  • A unique, classless development path rewards long-term character investment
  • The relationship with a Secian bondmate should meaningfully shape motivations, loyalties, and roleplay
  • Dragons often operate outside the social norms followed by shorter-lived races
  • Long lifespans, physical transformation, lair-building, and growing power offer distinctive story opportunities
  • Fully grown dragons are apex predators whose presence can reshape an encounter or community
