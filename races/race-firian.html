Overview

Fir Elves (Firians) possess a tanish skin tone—darker than Usilin but lighter than Sanene elves. They are notably athletic with a strong preference for outdoor living and demonstrate a great love for anything that has to do with nature. Superior with missile weapons, especially bows, they are the archetypal forest elves of Aradath.

Culture & Society

Firians demonstrate a great love for anything that has to do with nature. This deep connection to the natural world defines their culture, lifestyle, and values.

Racial Relations

Firians maintain friendly relations with most Aradath races but show particular comfort with Anthians. However, they harbor a strong hatred for the Sanene (San Elves), creating a deep divide within the elven races.

  • Friendly with: Most races, especially Anthians
  • Strong hatred for: Sanene (San Elves)
  • Neutral to: Other elven races

Lifestyle Preferences

  • Prefer outdoor living to cities
  • Drawn to forests and wilderness
  • Athletic and active lifestyle
  • Connection to nature is spiritual and practical

The Three Elven Races

Firians are one of three elven races in Aradath, each with distinct characteristics:

Fir Elves (Firian)

Forest dwellers, nature lovers, superior archers with tanish skin

Usil Elves (Usilin)

Scholarly, intellectual, palest of the elves, focused on magic

San Elves (Sanene)

Violent, skilled in magic and combat, darkest skin, untrustworthy
