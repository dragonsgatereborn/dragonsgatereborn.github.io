Overview

Born of chaos, the San Elves are a people of turbulence and power. Their origins are unclear, though early records place them in the earlier days of recorded history. The race is centralized on the Isle of Muldavia, home of the Muatana-al, where the Sanene live and maneuver within a harsh power structure.

San Elves are found wherever chaos and power gather. They resemble other elves but are thought by Usil scholars to be creations of Chaos rather than true elven kin.

Background

San Elves prize power above all: power of magick, weapons, information, and manipulation. Even those closest to them may be used to achieve their ends.

They are often found within temples, governments, and elite circles, where their drive for control outweighs any dislike for those they deem beneath them.

Temperament

San Elves are the black sheep of the elven races. They are conniving, power-hungry, and quick to anger. They embrace destruction and death if it serves their purpose.

They are true followers of Chaos and will do whatever it takes to gain power and knowledge.

Tendencies

San Elves scoff at scholarly Usil Elves and forest-dwelling Fir Elves. Manipulation is common, and destruction is acceptable if it harms others.

Traits

Advantages: Night vision, excellent weapon and magick users. Strong for elves. Very intelligent.

Hindrances: Untrustworthy and devoted only to serving themselves and Chaos.

Abilities: Magick (capable but not as skilled as Usil), Weapons (agile and strong), a knack for causing trouble.

Alignment: Entropic, Chaotic

Professions: Cleric, Rune Mage, Thief. Also: Fighter, Barbarian. Rare: Bard, Psion.

Role-Play Considerations

  • Power-seeking and manipulation should drive interactions
  • Lean into chaos, ambition, and quick tempers
  • Use intellect and agility to control situations
