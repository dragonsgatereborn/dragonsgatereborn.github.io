Overview

Drag-als are short, dragon-like beings who possess the power to breathe fire but lack the claws and wings of true dragons. These jovial creatures are known for their love of jokes, strong ale, and storytelling. While they avoid laborious work, they form friendships readily and possess superior bargaining skills that make them natural traders and performers.

Abilities & Traits

Strengths:

  • Fire-breathing capability
  • Night vision
  • Natural scale armor provides protection
  • Knowledge of additional languages
  • Superior bargaining skills
  • Form friendships readily
  • Draconic heritage and appearance

Limitations:

  • Small stature creates combat challenges
  • Lack claws and wings of true dragons
  • Avoid laborious work
  • Viewed as "loud and obnoxious" by Monitanians

Culture & Society

Drag-als are jovial creatures with a vibrant social culture centered around humor, strong drink, and storytelling. They possess a natural charisma that makes them excellent performers and traders, though their aversion to hard labor means they gravitate toward professions that rely on wit and skill rather than physical strength.

Social Nature

Drag-als love:

  • Telling and listening to jokes
  • Strong ale and beverages
  • Stories and tales of adventure
  • Making friends and socializing
  • Bargaining and negotiation

Racial Relations

While Drag-als form friendships readily with most races, Monitanians view them as "loud and obnoxious" due to their boisterous nature and tendency to avoid serious work.
