RIPOFF
Author: Command Master
Keywords: ripoff rip off
Type: Command

Syntax: ripoff <target> - tries to rip off a 'someone' disguise from a player.


The ripoff command tries to remove a disguise from a target.

Ripoff may only be used on a target every once in awhile. The success rate depends on the
attributes of both players.

See: help class ats.commands.informative.ScoreCommand, help disguise

OPEN
Author: Command Master
Keywords: open 
Type: Command

Syntax: open <item>|<slot> [room|inv|eq] - opens the item.
Using 'room', 'inv', or 'eq' will ensure an item will only be from that location (the room or your inventory or eq). Optional.

ORACLE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: oracle elder staff gm gamemaster guide assist
Type: Command

Syntax: oracle <message> - sends a help message to staff members. If there are no staff members on, this will send an email to Dayrinni.
    An email can only be sent every 30 seconds (game wide).
    In addition, all oracles are saved per server session so staff can see them upon logging into the game at a later date!


The oracle command allows a player to send a Helper and Staff wide help message. Any active
Helper and Staff will see this message and assist you.

If there are no staff online, a notification will be sent and the message logged for when a
staff member enters the game.

You may email Dayrinni(dayrinni@tempestseason.com) for assistance.

You may also try other help files which could provide assistance: channels, feedback,


discord, stuck.

See: help class ats.commands.communication.NewbieChatCommand, help feedback, help channels,
help discord, help stuck

ORDER
Author: Command Master
Keywords: order 
Type: Command

Syntax: order <player>|<longID>|all <command> - orders NPC(s) to do a command. To find longID, use call list
Syntax: order <player>|<longID>|all kill <target> - orders NPC(s) to attack the target. To find longID, use call list
Syntax: order <player>|all stop - cancels any current combat action.
This command is logged. Abuse is NOT tolerated! Pets do not save their inventory and any given items CAN BE LOST!


The order command allows control of pets and other followers.

To have your pets and followers attack a target, order them to kill <target>

For example, if a pet can speak, you could make it say something like so: order thug say
Hey there.

This command is logged. Abuse is NOT tolerated! Pets do not save their inventory and any
given items CAN BE LOST!

See: help call, help role play

OOG-RA
Type: Race
Keywords: Oog-ra
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Barbarian
Racial Tensions: Thugian
Racial Abilities: None

The Oog-ra are extremely large, hairy humanoids with sloping foreheads and protruding
brows. Their massive frames feature sunken eyes and heavy fur, making them among the most
physically imposing beings in the lands. According to legend, the Oog-ra originated from
the icy Northern Isles, specifically a remote mountain range near Crescent Lake, and their
origins allegedly trace back to the Lords of Chaos.

While possessing incredible strength, the Oog-ra are widely regarded as the least
intelligent race. They are ruled by instinct rather than intellect, and though they can be
violent, they lack malicious intent. Their simple nature makes them easily manipulated and
often exploited by other races. Historical records show the Frontacian Empire once debated
exterminating them, and later attempted selective breeding to increase their intelligence.
Oog-ras enjoy destruction, particularly loud activities like smashing things, and are most
commonly found serving as Fighters and Barbarians.

To learn more about the Oog-ra please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-oogra.html

MATERIALS
Type: Materials
Keywords: Materials,Orinsteel,Platinum,Titanium,Karnite,Miltanium,Metal,Iron Works,Forge,Ore,Ingot,Smelt

A Tempest Season there are several types of rare materials used for high leveled weapons,
armor and shields.

These materials are required to purchase gear upgrades in the Tralisia Merchant District.

1. Acquire a piece of ore: trace or chunk.
2. Go to the Iron Works and have a smith refine the pieces into ingots by using the BUY command.


3. Take the refined ingots to the desired store, weapon or armor, and purchase the item.

Tips for finding ore and ingots is as follows:
Levels 40-69: Orinsteel - Cave and Lake Walkers
Levels 70-100: Platinum - Basin Worms and Ragweed Beasts (best drop rate)
Levels 100: Titanium - Hydras
Levels 100+: Karnite - Dead Oak
Levels 100+: Miltanium - Completing the quest Protect Tralisia: Destroy the Dead Oak Terror Knight and fighting frostbit trolls, ice spiders, and frost wyverns
Levels 100+: Wind-metal and Lightning-metal - Tower of Windis and Lita and the Hall of Elements in the Merchant Distrct
Levels 100+: Frost-metal - Ice Prison and the Hall of Elements in the Merchant Distrct 

The city of Tralisia is always looking to improve their supply and processing of ore and
events occur to do just this so stay tuned!
