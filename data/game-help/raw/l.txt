GROUPPROTOTYPE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: groupprototype party leader
Type: Command

Syntax: group - checks the group status.
Syntax: group <target> - add a player who is following you to your group. Must be the group leader and the target must be in the same room.
Syntax: group leader <target> - gives group leader to the target. Must be the group leader and the target must be in the same room.
Syntax: group combine <target> - tries to combine the target's group with yours. Target must be leader and in the same room.
Syntax: group accept <player> - accepts a request to combine your group from the player. Target must be leader and in the same room.
Syntax: group allFollowers - groups all followers currently in the same room as you.
Syntax: group forceGroup <player> - forces this player to join your group and makes them stand (only active during Quest Mode)


The group command forms a party from the people currently following you.

While in a group, all experience and ability points are shared. Vitals of the group can be
seen by typing 'group'.

Group members also gain access to a special channel, gsay, which sends IC messages to all
other players in the group regardless of location.

See: help follow, help disband, help nofollow, help groupchat,

The look command allows you to examine other players, NPCs, or items in the world.

UNLOCK
Author: Command Master
Keywords: unlock lockpick lock pick
Type: Command

Syntax: unlock <item>|<slot> [room|inv|eq]
Using 'room', 'inv', or 'eq' will ensure an item will only be from that location (the room or your inventory or eq). Optional.


The unlock command attempts to unlock a container(chest, box, etc) or portal(gate, door,
etc). Some items require keys to be unlocked, others do not.

For containers, there are three ways to unlock it:
using the key (if one exists
asking your neighborhood Rogue to pick the lock
visiting Hoss at his Locksmith Shop in the alley behind the southern section of Merchant Distirct

For portals, there are two ways to unlock it:
using the key (if one exists
asking your neighborhood Rogue to pick the lock

Not all locks can be picked.

For unlocking affects on magic items, see help detectmagic

See: help locksmith, help lock, help open, help close, help picklocks, help detectmagic

LOCKSMITH
Author: Command Master
Keywords: locksmith 
Type: Command

Syntax: locksmith <item> - asks the locksmith to unlock this item.
Syntax: locksmith all [empty] - asks the locksmith to unlock and open all your locked items. If 'empty' is used he buys the chests and gives you the items.


The locksmith command, when performed at a locksmith, unlocks all containers for a small
fee per chest.

The locksmith can be found in the Spur Merchant District: all south, through the alley, all
east, through the door.

It is better to seek out a Rogue to perform unlocking services as much more gold will be
gained!

See: help open, help unlock, help close, help lock, help rogue, help pick lock
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PAY
Author: Command Master
Keywords: pay bounty wanted law pay jail criminal
Type: Command

Syntax: pay bounty - tries to pay your bounty to remove the WANTED flag. You must be at a bounty office.
Syntax: pay check - views the cost to remove your bounty. You must be at a bounty office.


The pay command pays off a bounty (WANTED flag) gained from attacking the guards, pick
pocketing or similar illegal activities.

You can only pay a bounty at the bounty office, Northwest from the center of Spur.

The cost of the bounty scales based on character level.

Being Wanted will cause the city guards to attack on sight so be careful while moving int
the city!

LEARN
Author: Command Master


Keywords: learn 
Type: Command

learn list - lists the abilities and specializations that can be learned at this location.
learn ability <slot> - learns the ability from the trainer for a fee.
learn spec <slot> - learns the specialization from the trainer for a fee.
learn fromItem|item <item name>|<slot> - scribes the knowledge from an item in your inventory, which may give new specializations or abilities.

See help learn for more information.

To train existing abilities, see the train command.


The learn command learns a new ability or specialization at the trainer.

Abilities can be learned at any level at the trainer.

Specializations can be learned starting at level 25 at the trainer.

(Mages learn from Scrolls and Blood Shamans from Totems found in the wild).

LEUIAN
Type: Race
Keywords: Leuian
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Thief, Rune Mage, Psion
Racial Tensions: They form their own opinions of each race as they play
Racial Abilities: None

Leuians are fierce warriors, considered some of the most fearsome in the lands. These
feline-humanoid beings feature retractable claws, tails, fur, and walk upright. They live
by The Precepts, a strict ethical code serving as their law, emphasizing honor, truth, and
direct engagement while disdaining deception, theft, and dishonesty.

To learn more about the Leuian please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-leuian.html


See: help <ability name>, help <class name>, help races
