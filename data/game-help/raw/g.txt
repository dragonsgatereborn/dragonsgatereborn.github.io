seen by typing 'group'.

other players in the group regardless of location.

TRAIN
Author: Command Master
Keywords: train trainer guildlist classlist raise
Type: Command

Syntax: train list - lists the abilities and specializations that can be trained at this location.
Syntax: train <slot> - trains the ability up to the next level. This will require a tier point and sometimes an item (the train will inform you on what is needed).
Syntax: train spec <slot> - trains the specialization up to the next level. This may require a tier point and a small fee.

Syntax: train tier cost - checks the cost of the next tier point you may buy.
Syntax: train tier buy - buys a tier point for the associated gold price.
Syntax: train tier respec confirm - resets all of your tier points for 5,000 gold. All of the abilities and specializations you trained will be set to lv 1. You will receive a 75% refund of your ability points.

Syntax: train guildlist - views your standing in your classes guild.
Syntax: train history - views your training attribute gain history.


The train command raises a level of an ability, specialization, or aptitude to the next
level.

The cost of raising to the next level varies:


1. Possibly using a TIER POINT and/or money, if applicable to raise an ability in level.
2. Using a TIER point (and money, if applicable) to raise a specialization in level.

The money cost to raise an ability up a level depends on the amount of uses the ability has
left.

You gain tier points upon leveling up.

You can also buy tier points at the trainer using 'train tier buy'.

Respeccing your tier points will set all of your non-natural abilities to their initial
level.

To learn more about training and learning new abilities visit the manual:
www.tempestseason.com/manual/abilities/#Training_Abilities_and_Specializations

See: help learn, help class ats.commands.PracticeCommand, help class
ats.commands.informative.SpecializationsCommand, help tier points, help anima convergence

GUARD
Author: Command Master
Keywords: guard defend
Type: Command

Syntax: guard <target>


The guard command allows you to attempt to draw a monster's attacks away from someone, and
onto yourself. The attempt always works, as long as you are close enough to step in front
of them.

Note: Monsters choose who they wish to target each attack and may revert back to the
defended target if they attacked the monster again or if the monster is aggressive,

There are abilities which lock a monsters target to you and are more effective than guard.

GO-BLIN
Type: Race
Keywords: Go-blin
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Bard, Thief, Barbarian, Forester, Rune Mage, Psion
Racial Tensions: They form their own opinions of each race as they play
Racial Abilities: None

Go-blins are small, wretched creatures that dwell in dark cave systems, organized into
tight-knit clans. Standing only three to four feet tall, they possess a distinctive warty
green hide covered in pulsating yellow warts, broken tusks, and filthy skin. Their
uniformly black eyes peer out from faces universally considered repulsive, featuring
mottled green or grey skin with various visible deformities.

These nocturnal beings are compulsive hoarders, accumulating refuse and items of
questionable value from raids and scavenging expeditions. Spitting serves as a common
greeting among their kind, and they actively avoid sunlight whenever possible. Go-blins are
punks and bullies by nature, sadistic when the balance is in their favor, yet cowardly when
facing genuine threats. They prey on weaker creatures but scatter when confronted by
superior force, valuing cowardice as a survival mechanism and disdaining those who stand
and fight needlessly.

To learn more about the Go-blin please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-goblin.html

GO-BLIN-AL
Type: Race
Keywords: Go-blin-al
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Thief, Barbarian, Rune Mage
Racial Tensions: They form their own opinions of each race as they play
Racial Abilities: None

Go-blin-als are believed to be servants to Chaos, possibly created through the warped
experiments of the Chaos Lords. Scholars suggest they may be remnants of a formerly mighty


race, corrupted and twisted by dark magic into the ugly, mottled green creatures seen
today. Standing approximately three feet tall, these bipedal beings are typically filthy
and foul-tempered, dwelling in caves within tight-knit clans.

Nasty, violent, and full of mischief, Go-blin-als are bullies and punks who become sadistic
when they hold the advantage but turn cowardly when facing genuine threats. They avoid
sunlight and delight in harassing weaker creatures. Spitting at each other is a common form
of greeting among their kind. They speak with distinctive lisp-like patterns and an
underdeveloped grasp of Common.

Go-blin-als possess natural night vision, quickness, agility, and dexterity, though they
suffer from a troublemaker reputation, physical weakness, and communication barriers with
other races. Their chaotic alignment draws them primarily to the profession of Thief, with
rare individuals becoming Fighters, Barbarians, or Rune Mages.

To learn more about the Go-blin-al please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-goblinal.html
