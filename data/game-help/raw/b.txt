BOX
Author: Command Master
Keywords: box 
Type: Command

Syntax: box store <item>|<slot> - stores the item stack in the bank vault
Syntax: box store <#> <item>|<slot> - stores a number of items from your inventory
Syntax: box remove <item>|<slot> - retrieves the item stack from your bank vault
Syntax: box view
Syntax: box upgrade yes|cost - views the cost of the upgrade or purchases an upgrade


The box command allows you to manage your stored items in the bank's vault: storing and
removing items.

The vault has a limited amount of spaces for items, and items inside containers are counted
towards the total.

Upgrades may be purchased and the size of the bank vault is infinite.

See: help bank, help inventory, help vault, help cache, help currency

PAY
Author: Command Master


Keywords: pay bounty wanted law pay jail criminal
Type: Command

Syntax: pay bounty - tries to pay your bounty to remove the WANTED flag. You must be at a bounty office.
Syntax: pay check - views the cost to remove your bounty. You must be at a bounty office.


The pay command pays off a bounty (WANTED flag) gained from attacking the guards, pick
pocketing or similar illegal activities.

You can only pay a bounty at the bounty office, Northwest from the center of Spur.

The cost of the bounty scales based on character level.

Being Wanted will cause the city guards to attack on sight so be careful while moving int
the city!

BREAK
Author: Command Master
Keywords: break 
Type: Command

Syntax: break <item>|<slot> - destroys an item. Kept and Retained items cannot be broken.
Syntax: break stack <item>|<slot> - destroys all items at the stack. Kept and Retained items cannot be broken.


The break command destroys an item - removing it from the game forever.

To prevent an item from being broken, mark it KEEP or RETAIN.

Note: While selling, there is no need to break empty containers as they can be sold
on-mass.

See: help keep, help retain, help sell,

BARD
Type: Class
Keywords: Bard
Difficulty: MEDIUM
Role: Utility, Control, Healer
Allowed Races: Anthian, Drag-al, Fir Elf, Flerian, Frontacian, Go-blin, Hithual, Human,
Psycian, San Elf, Usilin
Specializations: None

Bards are versatile entertainers capable of casting spells and combat, though their magical
abilities are less powerful than dedicated spell-casters. They gain some thief-like
abilities including disguise and excel at performance-based roleplay. The class suits
players who value versatility, charm, and creative problem-solving within group dynamics.


To learn more about the Bard please visit the web page:

BARBARIAN
Type: Class
Keywords: Barbarian
Difficulty: EASY
Role: Damage, Tank
Allowed Races: Anthian, Arachnian, Drag-al, Flerian, Go-blin, Go-blin-al, Hithual, Human,
Monitanian, Muatana-al, Oog-ra, Psycian, San Elf, Thugian
Specializations: None

Barbarians are powerful melee combatants distinguished by their signature Berserk ability.
When sufficiently wounded in combat, they enter an uncontrolled frenzy that dramatically


amplifies their damage output and attack speed. However, this berserker state comes at a
cost - they lose control and become consumed by combat instinct. Once the frenzy ends,
severe fatigue follows.


To learn more about the Barbarian please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/classes/class-barbarian.html
