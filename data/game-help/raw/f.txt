FIRST AID
Type: Skill
Keywords: First Aid firstaid
Character Level Required: All Classes(1). 
Maximum Training Level: 1. Range: 10ft

Syntax: use firstaid <target> - applies first aid to heal a target's wounds

First Aid.

First Aid (single victim): applies first aid to heal wounds (+chance, +potency, -delay).

The target must be sitting or lying down to receive first aid. Healing scales from First
Aid level, Wisdom, and Dexterity. Result quality ranges from Partial to Critical, with a
hard cap of 35 on critical heals.

TRAINING KEY: potency - damage/effectiveness, chance - chance of failure/success, delay -
length of ability delay on use

FIRE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: fire volley
Type: Command

Syntax: fire <rapid|deftly|precisely> <target> - fires with the indicated attack. (Can abbr the attack types, ie: r for rapid)
Syntax: fire <direction> <range> - fires a volley in the indicated direction.
Syntax: fire artillery [room cannon] <target> - fires the equipped or room artillery at the target. If the target is out of range, the cannon will still fire.


The fire command is used to fire bows, crossbows and firearms at a target. The weapon
generally requires some form of ammo required, such as a quiver with arrows or bolts, or a
magazine in a fire arm. Bows are automatically loaded from the equipped quiver on each
fire. Crossbows and firearms require a manual reload. Ranged weapons can also be unloaded,
if needed.

Bows recieve a 50% damage bonus if used outside of melee range(2 units (10 feet)) of a
target. Advance or retreat before battle to do maximum damage.

Bows can also be volleyed into other rooms, dealing damage from afar. Damage is reduced by
25%. Be warned, any struck targets will chase after you after taking fire!

To see the differences between rapid, deftly and precisely type: attackinfo

See: help advance, help retreat, help move, help reload, help unload, help class
ats.commands.informative.AttackInfoCommand, help ranger, help risk

FEEL
Author: Command Master
Keywords: feel 
Type: Command

Syntax: feel <message> - sends an in character feel message to all staff members.
This is great for RP as it assists the staff in moving events along for your character!

The feel command allows your character to have a private feelings, which is sent to all
Staff members.

Feeling during any role play is vital (and highly encouraged) to your characters
development because it allows the Staff to be notified of active RP and also non-obvious
information.

Knowing what a character is feeling can allow the staff to move the quest into a more
meaningful direction. For example: a character may be willing to con a NPC but aren't sure


if it is possible. A thought about this can give the Staff the information they need to
open up this line of RP!

Periodically, bonus experience and other RP rewards are given out to characters who use the
feel command often.

Note: Other characters may be able to sense feelings.

This is a role play command - use it to enhance your character!

See: help think, help socials, help emote, help smote, help pose, help wmote, help mood,
help role play

FIR ELF
Type: Race
Keywords: Fir Elf
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Bard, Forester
Racial Tensions: San Elf
Racial Abilities: None

The Firians, also known as Fir Elves, are forest elves deeply connected to the natural
world. They possess a great love for nature and are renowned for their exceptional archery
skills. Athletic in build, Firians prefer wilderness and outdoor living over urban
environments, blending effectively into shadows and the forest canopy. They are gifted with
night vision, making them formidable in low-light conditions. Firians are known as superior
marksmen, wielding missile weapons with unmatched proficiency, particularly bows. Their
foresters gain additional skinning bonuses, reflecting their deep practical knowledge of
the wild. While physically weaker in melee combat compared to more combat-focused races,
their agility and ranged prowess more than compensate. Firians maintain friendly relations
with most races, particularly the Anthians, but harbor a strong hatred toward the Sanene,
their dark elven kin.

To learn more about the Fir Elf please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-firian.html

FLERIAN
Type: Race
Keywords: Flerian
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary


Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Bard, Thief, Barbarian, Forester, Rune Mage, Psion
Racial Tensions: They form their own opinions of each race as they play
Racial Abilities: None

Flerians are short, mildly furry, slightly built humanoids with long dexterous fingers who
live in communal underground societies called burrows. They are friendly, curious,
talkative, and often dramatic, with short attention spans and a light-hearted nature. Their
narrow, sharp features are complemented by large, round eyes that grant them exceptional
night vision.

Known for their remarkable agility and dexterity, Flerians move almost continuously and are
notoriously difficult to restrain. They have a well-earned reputation for grabbing anything
that is not nailed down, which often creates conflicts in urban areas. Despite caring
little for material possessions, they are fiercely loyal to their burrow and close
companions. Members of this race appear quite similar to one another to outsiders, making
individual identification a challenge for other races.

To learn more about the Flerian please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-flerian.html

FRONTACIAN
Type: Race
Keywords: Frontacian
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Bard, Rune Mage
Racial Tensions: Arachnian
Racial Abilities: None

Frontacians are one of the oldest and most influential races in Aradath, having ruled the
greatest single Empire the world has ever seen. During the Second and Third Ages they built
magickal bridges that linked the realm, and their name translates to "Jeweled Ones" owing
to the three embedded jewel-like formations found on their foreheads and palms. Tall and
pale with white hair and eyes, Frontacians view themselves as elite politicians, thinkers,
and runemages with aspirations for renewed dominance.

The race possesses exceptional magical talent, legendary arcane creations, and the ability
to drain energy from others. However, Frontacians suffer from a genetic malady tied to the
Great Unmagick, requiring a weekly medication derived almost entirely from dragon essence.
Missing a single dose causes instability, and two missed doses risk fatality. Physically
fragile compared to martial races, they compensate with unmatched intellect, cunning, and


raw magical power. Frontacians exhibit considerable imperial pride and a cold, uncaring
demeanor toward those they consider lesser, though honesty holds significant cultural value
within their own society.

To learn more about the Frontacian please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-frontacian.html

MATERIALS
Type: Materials
Keywords: Materials,Orinsteel,Platinum,Titanium,Karnite,Miltanium,Metal,Iron Works,Forge,Ore,Ingot,Smelt

A Tempest Season there are several types of rare materials used for high leveled weapons,
armor and shields.

These materials are required to purchase gear upgrades in the Tralisia Merchant District.

1. Acquire a piece of ore: trace or chunk.


2. Go to the Iron Works and have a smith refine the pieces into ingots by using the BUY command.
3. Take the refined ingots to the desired store, weapon or armor, and purchase the item.

Tips for finding ore and ingots is as follows:
Levels 40-69: Orinsteel - Cave and Lake Walkers
Levels 70-100: Platinum - Basin Worms and Ragweed Beasts (best drop rate)
Levels 100: Titanium - Hydras
Levels 100+: Karnite - Dead Oak
Levels 100+: Miltanium - Completing the quest Protect Tralisia: Destroy the Dead Oak Terror Knight and fighting frostbit trolls, ice spiders, and frost wyverns
Levels 100+: Wind-metal and Lightning-metal - Tower of Windis and Lita and the Hall of Elements in the Merchant Distrct
Levels 100+: Frost-metal - Ice Prison and the Hall of Elements in the Merchant Distrct 

The city of Tralisia is always looking to improve their supply and processing of ore and
events occur to do just this so stay tuned!
