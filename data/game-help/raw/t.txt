CALL
Author: Command Master
Keywords: call pets animal tag mounts
Type: Command

Syntax: call <item> - summons the pet on a pet tag or mount on a mount tag!
Syntax: call <slot> - summons the pet on a pet tag or mount on a mount tag!
Syntax: call patreonPet - summons the patreon pet, if one is made. Use the Patreon command to create a pet!
Syntax: call patreonMount - summons and mounts the patreon mount, if one is made. Use the Patreon command to create a mount! (Tier 5)

Syntax: call dismiss [longID]|all - dismisses your current pet. If the tag was a single use, this pet will be lost forever.
Syntax: call list - List the pets and their LongID so you can dismiss or interact with them via order.
Syntax: call pets - calls for your already existing pets and has them returns to you! This won't work if the pets are in combat.
Syntax: call resonance - summons your already existing resonance to you!


The call command summons a mount, a new pet, dismisses a pet, or returns a lost pet to your
side.

Typically, pets and mounts must be purchased or acquired through a role play event. They
can then be summoned with the call command and controlled with the order command.

Pets can perish and be lost forever. Be careful of giving them rare and expensive items as
they can be easily lost as pet inventory is not saved. Mounts do not perish and last
forever.

See: help order, help class ats.commands.items.PatreonCommand, help dismount, help role
play

TRAIN
Author: Command Master
Keywords: train trainer guildlist classlist raise
Type: Command

Syntax: train list - lists the abilities and specializations that can be trained at this location.
Syntax: train <slot> - trains the ability up to the next level. This will require a tier point and sometimes an item (the train will inform you on what is needed).
Syntax: train spec <slot> - trains the specialization up to the next level. This may require a tier point and a small fee.


Syntax: train tier cost - checks the cost of the next tier point you may buy.
Syntax: train tier buy - buys a tier point for the associated gold price.
Syntax: train tier respec confirm - resets all of your tier points for 5,000 gold. All of the abilities and specializations you trained will be set to lv 1. You will receive a 75% refund of your ability points.

Syntax: train guildlist - views your standing in your classes guild.
Syntax: train history - views your training attribute gain history.


The train command raises a level of an ability, specialization, or aptitude to the next
level.

The cost of raising to the next level varies:
1. Possibly using a TIER POINT and/or money, if applicable to raise an ability in level.
2. Using a TIER point (and money, if applicable) to raise a specialization in level.

The money cost to raise an ability up a level depends on the amount of uses the ability has
left.

You gain tier points upon leveling up.

You can also buy tier points at the trainer using 'train tier buy'.

Respeccing your tier points will set all of your non-natural abilities to their initial
level.

To learn more about training and learning new abilities visit the manual:
www.tempestseason.com/manual/abilities/#Training_Abilities_and_Specializations

See: help learn, help class ats.commands.PracticeCommand, help class
ats.commands.informative.SpecializationsCommand, help tier points, help anima convergence

SAY
Author: Command Master
Keywords: say speech talk
Type: Command

Syntax: say <message> - speaks in the current language and sends the message to the current room.
Syntax: say <NAME, message> - speaks in the current language and sends the message to the current room, addressing the target named NAME.


The say command communicates with other players and NPCs in the immediate room.

By default, you speak the common language, which all other races can understand and speak.
This can be changed by using the language command.


You may target a specific person to speak too by typing: say TARGET, MESSAGE. Example: say Dayrinni, Hello there!
Note the comma after the name.

Adult and Elder dragons can speak while sleeping.

See: help language, help whisper, help shout, help groupchat, help channels

THINK


Author: Command Master
Keywords: think 
Type: Command

Syntax: think <message> - sends an in character think message to all staff members.
This is great for RP as it assists the staff in moving events along for your character!

The think command allows your character to have a private thought, which is sent to all
Staff members.

Thinking during any role play is vital (and highly encouraged) to your characters
development because it allows the Staff to be notified of active RP and also non-obvious
information.

Knowing what a character is thinking can allow the staff to move the quest into a more
meaningful direction. For example: a character may be willing to con a NPC but aren't sure
if it is possible. A thought about this can give the Staff the information they need to
open up this line of RP!

Periodically, bonus experience and other RP rewards are given out to characters who use the
think command often.

Note: Other characters, such as those with psionics, are able to hear thoughts.

This is a role play command - use it to enhance your character!

See: help feel, help socials, help emote, help smote, help pose, help wmote, help mood,
help role play

THUGIAN
Type: Race
Keywords: Thugian
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Thief, Barbarian, Rune Mage
Racial Tensions: They form their own opinions of each race as they play
Racial Abilities: None

Thugians are massive, brutish humanoids renowned for their exceptional physical power and
remarkable ability to survive. Considered among the strongest of all humanoid races, these
towering beings combine tremendous muscle with an almost supernatural capacity for rapid
healing, recovering from wounds that would fell lesser creatures. Their intelligence,
however, is severely limited, and their communication skills are poor at best.

Despite their fearsome reputation, Thugians have simple needs. They are almost constantly
hungry and will consume whatever is readily available, including the flesh of other
sentient beings, with particular fondness for humans and trolls. They are easily
entertained and attracted to small, shiny, or cute objects, becoming fiercely possessive of
anything that catches their eye. A well-fed Thugian can become a surprisingly loyal
companion, though their chaotic nature and unpredictable temperament make their anger
difficult to foresee. Preferred professions include Fighter and Barbarian, while Thief and
Rune Mage are exceedingly rare among their kind.

To learn more about the Thugian please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-thugian.html

MATERIALS
Type: Materials
Keywords: Materials,Orinsteel,Platinum,Titanium,Karnite,Miltanium,Metal,Iron Works,Forge,Ore,Ingot,Smelt

A Tempest Season there are several types of rare materials used for high leveled weapons,
armor and shields.


These materials are required to purchase gear upgrades in the Tralisia Merchant District.

1. Acquire a piece of ore: trace or chunk.
2. Go to the Iron Works and have a smith refine the pieces into ingots by using the BUY command.
3. Take the refined ingots to the desired store, weapon or armor, and purchase the item.

Tips for finding ore and ingots is as follows:
Levels 40-69: Orinsteel - Cave and Lake Walkers
Levels 70-100: Platinum - Basin Worms and Ragweed Beasts (best drop rate)
Levels 100: Titanium - Hydras
Levels 100+: Karnite - Dead Oak
Levels 100+: Miltanium - Completing the quest Protect Tralisia: Destroy the Dead Oak Terror Knight and fighting frostbit trolls, ice spiders, and frost wyverns
Levels 100+: Wind-metal and Lightning-metal - Tower of Windis and Lita and the Hall of Elements in the Merchant Distrct
Levels 100+: Frost-metal - Ice Prison and the Hall of Elements in the Merchant Distrct 

The city of Tralisia is always looking to improve their supply and processing of ore and
events occur to do just this so stay tuned!

The following materials and their respective tiers are as follows:

 -  wood (tier 1)
 -  copper (tier 2)
 -  bone (tier 3)
 -  bronze (tier 4)
 -  iron (tier 5)
 -  steel (tier 6)
 -  orinsteel (tier 7)

 -  titanium (tier 9)

 -  karnite (tier 10)

Higher tiers mean stronger damage and protection.
