EMOTE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: emote 
Type: Command

Syntax: emote <message> - displays the message to the room, with your name first.


Emote displays a message to the room for role play purposes. Your name is highlighted in
yellow and quoted speech is highlighted in green. You can use $c to insert your capitalized
name.

Example:
emote jumps up on the table
Would show up as:
Dayrinni jumps up on the table.

A message that opens with punctuation runs straight on from your name, so possessives and
asides read naturally. A message that does not already end in . ? or ! gets a period.
Example:
emote 's eyes narrow
Would show up as:
Dayrinni's eyes narrow.

You can insert your character's pronouns based on your sex: $e (he/she), $s (his/her), and
$m (him/her).
Example:
emote grins and cracks $s knuckles.
Would show up as (for a male character):
Dayrinni grins and cracks his knuckles.

Capitalized $E (he/she), $S (his/her), and $M (him/her) insert your target's pronouns
instead - the nearest player or NPC you name with @ before the token.
Example:
emote claps @john on the back as $E grins.
Would show up as (if John is male):
Dayrinni claps John on the back as he grins.

You can target players, NPCs, or objects using the @ prefix, which is replaced with the
target's name.
Example:
emote looks at @john and waves at @sword
Would show up as:


Dayrinni looks at John and waves at an iron sword.

Targets are searched in this order: players/NPCs in the room, inventory, equipment, then
room objects.

Punctuation after a target is kept, so you can write possessives and asides straight
through.
Example:
emote looks at @john's red eyes, then at @sword...
Would show up as:
Dayrinni looks at John's red eyes, then at an iron sword...

For precise targeting, use a prefix modifier:
@name - searches all (player/NPC, then inventory, equipment, room)
@self or @me - your own name, so a line can refer back to you
@p:name - player/NPC only
@i:name - inventory only (also supports slot: @i:2 for slot 2)
@e:name - equipment only
@r:name - room only (also supports slot: @r:3 for slot 3)

See: help socials, help smote, help pose, help wmote, help mood, help think, help role play

SOCIALS
Author: Command Master
Keywords: socials emotes
Type: Command

Syntax: socials - lists all the socials you have access to.
Syntax: [sociial] <social name> - performs the social, ie: smile or social smile.
Syntax: [sociial] <social name> [target] - performs the social at the target, ie: smile dayrinni
Syntax: [sociial]  <social name> [target] [message] - performs the social at the target with the extra message, ie: smile dayrinni and pats his back


The socials command lists all of the socials (RP actions) you have access to.

Socials are actions used during RP, such as laughing, smirking, or crying. Many socials
accept targets and text on the end.
For example: smile dayrinni would smile at Dayrinni.
For example: smile dayrinni and then pats his back would smile at Dayrinni and then have your character pat his back.

See: help emote, help smote, help pose, help wmote, help mood, help think, help role play

FIR ELF
Type: Race
Keywords: Fir Elf
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Bard, Forester
Racial Tensions: San Elf
Racial Abilities: None

The Firians, also known as Fir Elves, are forest elves deeply connected to the natural
world. They possess a great love for nature and are renowned for their exceptional archery
skills. Athletic in build, Firians prefer wilderness and outdoor living over urban
environments, blending effectively into shadows and the forest canopy. They are gifted with
night vision, making them formidable in low-light conditions. Firians are known as superior
marksmen, wielding missile weapons with unmatched proficiency, particularly bows. Their
foresters gain additional skinning bonuses, reflecting their deep practical knowledge of
the wild. While physically weaker in melee combat compared to more combat-focused races,
their agility and ranged prowess more than compensate. Firians maintain friendly relations
with most races, particularly the Anthians, but harbor a strong hatred toward the Sanene,
their dark elven kin.

To learn more about the Fir Elf please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-firian.html

SAN ELF
Type: Race
Keywords: San Elf
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:


Order of the Hand, Bard, Thief, Barbarian, Rune Mage, Psion
Racial Tensions: Fir Elf, Usilin
Racial Abilities: None

The Sanene, also known as San Elves, are dark-skinned elves originating from the Isle of
Muldavia. Scholars theorize they are creations of Chaos rather than true elven descendants,
a distinction that sets them apart from other elven races. Shorter and more sinewy than
their Fir and Usil counterparts, the Sanene possess skin tones ranging from light golden to
deep dark brown, with predominantly black hair and dark eyes.

The Sanene prize power above all else: power of magick, weapons, information, and
manipulation. They are conniving, power-hungry, and quick to anger, embracing destruction
and death if it serves their purpose. Their alignment is fundamentally entropic and
chaotic, with a deep devotion to Chaos itself. Sanene often infiltrate positions of
influence within temples, governments, and elite circles, using their exceptional
intelligence and cunning to further their ambitions.

Among their advantages, the Sanene possess night vision, excellence in both weaponry and
magic, above-average strength for elves, and high intelligence. However, their inherently
untrustworthy and self-serving nature is their greatest hindrance, making lasting alliances
rare and tenuous.

To learn more about the San Elf please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-sanene.html
