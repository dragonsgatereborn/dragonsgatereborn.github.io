HIDE
Type: Skill
Keywords: Hide hide
Character Level Required: All Classes(1). 
Maximum Training Level: 1. Range: 10ft

Syntax: use hide - attempts to slip into the shadows

Hiding.

Hide (single victim): allows you to slip into the shadows (+chance, -delay).

Success depends on your Hiding skill, Stealth skill, Agility, and Dexterity. Room lighting,
nearby enemies, and combat status affect your chances. Actions like attacking, casting, or
manipulating objects will reveal you.

TRAINING KEY: chance - chance of failure/success, delay - length of ability delay on use

See: help skill

HITHUAL
Type: Race
Keywords: Hithual
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Bard, Barbarian, Psion
Racial Tensions: They form their own opinions of each race as they play
Racial Abilities: None

Hithuals are stout humanoids hailing from Aerhauven within the Great Divide mountain range.


Known as some of the finest miners in the lands, they possess above-average strength and an
unshakeable resolve. Their culture centers around mining, craftsmanship, and a deep loyalty
to family and clan.

Fun-loving, brave, proud, and stubborn, Hithuals show equal enthusiasm for hard work and
hearty leisure. They form acquaintances readily but develop lasting bonds with outsiders
mainly through shared combat experiences. Beards are worn as symbols of skill and respect
by accomplished warriors of any gender. They possess a significant natural resistance to
magic, though this same resistance prevents them from becoming Rune Mages.

Hithuals distrust non-traditional methods such as magic, thievery, or charity, firmly
believing that worthwhile accomplishments require genuine effort. They are also renowned
for their remarkable tolerance for ale and their love of practical jokes.

To learn more about the Hithual please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-hithual.html

HUMAN
Type: Race
Keywords: Human
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Bard, Thief, Barbarian, Forester, Rune Mage, Psion
Racial Tensions: They form their own opinions of each race as they play
Racial Abilities: 

Humans are the most adaptable people in Aradath, thriving in nearly every climate, city,
and profession. Their strength lies in flexibility — they learn quickly, switch roles with
ease, and build communities wherever they settle.

While they lack the extreme gifts or drawbacks of other races, Humans excel through drive,
curiosity, and the sheer variety of paths open to them. If you want a race that can grow
into almost any story, Humans are the perfect starting point.

To learn more about the Human please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-human.html
