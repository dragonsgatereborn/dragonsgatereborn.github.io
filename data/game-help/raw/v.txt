FIRE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: fire volley
Type: Command

Syntax: fire <rapid|deftly|precisely> <target> - fires with the indicated attack. (Can abbr the attack types, ie: r for rapid)
Syntax: fire <direction> <range> - fires a volley in the indicated direction.
Syntax: fire artillery [room cannon] <target> - fires the equipped or room artillery at the target. If the target is out of range, the cannon will still fire.


The fire command is used to fire bows, crossbows and firearms at a target. The weapon
generally requires some form of ammo required, such as a quiver with arrows or bolts, or a
magazine in a fire arm. Bows are automatically loaded from the equipped quiver on each
fire. Crossbows and firearms require a manual reload. Ranged weapons can also be unloaded,
if needed.

Bows recieve a 50% damage bonus if used outside of melee range(2 units (10 feet)) of a
target. Advance or retreat before battle to do maximum damage.

Bows can also be volleyed into other rooms, dealing damage from afar. Damage is reduced by
25%. Be warned, any struck targets will chase after you after taking fire!

To see the differences between rapid, deftly and precisely type: attackinfo

See: help advance, help retreat, help move, help reload, help unload, help class
ats.commands.informative.AttackInfoCommand, help ranger, help risk

INSPECT
Author: Command Master
Keywords: inspect value worth examine identify
Type: Command

Syntax: inspect <item>|<slot> [room|inv|eq] - inspects an item.
Syntax: inspect <item>|<slot> [contianer] [room|inv|eq] - inspects an item inside a container.
Using 'room', 'inv', or 'eq' will ensure an item will only be from that location (the room or your inventory or eq). Optional.


The inspect command displays detailed information about a specific item, such as weight,
key words, wear locations and so on.

In order to view magical properties on an item, an Erius' Magnificent Magnifying Glass must
be in your possession. Mages can also use Detect Magic on an item to view magical
properties.


This item can be purchased from Ally's Shop, The Adventurer's Nook, in the Spur Merchant
District.

See: help magnifying glass, help detect magic, help appraise, help haggle, help bargain,
help currency

VAULT
Author: Command Master
Keywords: vault 
Type: Command

Syntax: vault store <item>|<slot> - stores the item stack in the house vault
Syntax: vault store <#> <item>|<slot> - stores a number of items from your inventory
Syntax: vault remove <item>|<slot> - retrieves the item stack from your house vault
Syntax: vault view


The vault command allows you to manage your stored items in the house vault: storing and
removing items.

The vault has a has 150 spaces for items and items inside containers are counted towards
the total.

Upgrades may be not currently be purchased.
