MOVE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: move 
Type: Command

Syntax: move +/-<units> - advances or retreats in a room. 1 unit = 5 feet.
Syntax: move <target> - move towards the target.


The move command allows you to move forwards or backwards in a room by a number of units.
Engaged NPCs will try to ensure they are always in attack range. If using a ranged weapon,
it is best to advance or retreat prior to starting combat. You may see the current location
of all NPCs and players in a room by using the assess command.

See: help advance, help retreat, help assess, help fire, help ranger

CALL
Author: Command Master
Keywords: call pets animal tag mounts
Type: Command

Syntax: call <item> - summons the pet on a pet tag or mount on a mount tag!
Syntax: call <slot> - summons the pet on a pet tag or mount on a mount tag!
Syntax: call patreonPet - summons the patreon pet, if one is made. Use the Patreon command to create a pet!
Syntax: call patreonMount - summons and mounts the patreon mount, if one is made. Use the Patreon command to create a mount! (Tier 5)

Syntax: call dismiss [longID]|all - dismisses your current pet. If the tag was a single use, this pet will be lost forever.
Syntax: call list - List the pets and their LongID so you can dismiss or interact with them via order.
Syntax: call pets - calls for your already existing pets and has them returns to you! This won't work if the pets are in combat.
Syntax: call resonance - summons your already existing resonance to you!


The call command summons a mount, a new pet, dismisses a pet, or returns a lost pet to your
side.

Typically, pets and mounts must be purchased or acquired through a role play event. They
can then be summoned with the call command and controlled with the order command.

Pets can perish and be lost forever. Be careful of giving them rare and expensive items as
they can be easily lost as pet inventory is not saved. Mounts do not perish and last
forever.

See: help order, help class ats.commands.items.PatreonCommand, help dismount, help role
play

MOOD
Author: Command Master
Keywords: mood 
Type: Command

Syntax: mood <mood> - switches to a new mood.
Syntax: mood current - views the current mood.
Syntax: mood list - views all the moods you can use.


Your characters can display a variety of moods during the course of their life time. The
mood command helps with integrating that aspect directly into the game. Your mood is
viewable when anyone looks at you and when your mood is changed. It can be a simple yet
effective command for RP!

This is a role play command - use it to enhance your character!

The list of moods can be expended - please suggest new moods to Dayrinni.

MONITANIAN
Type: Race
Keywords: Monitanian
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Thief, Barbarian, Rune Mage
Racial Tensions: Flerian, Go-blin-al, Drag-al
Racial Abilities: None

Monitanians are large, scaled, upright-walking lizard-like beings supported by powerful
tails. Originating primarily from the Dismal Swamp, they remain mysterious about their
history, though historical records recognize them as among the world's bravest peoples.
They communicate through gestures, hisses, and grunts, and cannot speak the Common tongue
due to vocal limitations, though they are highly intelligent despite racial assumptions
otherwise.

Monitanians value honor and allegiance above all else. Friendships are forged through
battlefield experiences and endure permanently. They possess natural clawing and biting
attacks, thick scaled skin that provides inherent armor, and night vision capability. Known
for their exceptional strength and sturdy physiology, they prefer the heaviest armor
available and often become mercenaries for just causes.


To learn more about the Monitanian please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-monitanian.html

MUATANA-AL
Type: Race
Keywords: Muatana-al
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Thief, Barbarian, Rune Mage
Racial Tensions: San Elf
Racial Abilities: None

The origins of the Muatana-al remain shrouded in mystery. Some believe they were once
cursed nobility, while others claim the Chaos Lords created them as instruments of terror.
Primarily associated with the Isle of Muldavia, they dominate the San Elves and their
leader, the Muatana-al Lord of the Dernath Woods, has allegedly forged pacts with Dragons
and Wyverns.

Tall and gaunt, the Muatana-al possess unnaturally pale flesh that seems to glow faintly in
darkness, with luminous veins visible beneath the surface in hues of crimson, blue, green,
and violet. They are cold, calculating, and utterly without mercy — intelligent predators
and patient hunters capable of stalking prey for extended periods. Solitary by nature, they
form alliances only when mutually beneficial, having long since abandoned any semblance of
compassion or empathy.

The Muatana-al must regularly drain the life force of living creatures to sustain
themselves. Feeding requires physical contact, leaving victims weakened, aged, or
desiccated. Without feeding, a Muatana-al suffers progressive weakness and eventual death.
They are strictly nocturnal creatures — sunlight causes searing pain and prolonged exposure
is lethal. Their entire culture revolves around the cycle of night and day.

To learn more about the Muatana-al please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-muatanaal.html

MATERIALS
Type: Materials
Keywords: Materials,Orinsteel,Platinum,Titanium,Karnite,Miltanium,Metal,Iron Works,Forge,Ore,Ingot,Smelt


A Tempest Season there are several types of rare materials used for high leveled weapons,
armor and shields.

These materials are required to purchase gear upgrades in the Tralisia Merchant District.

1. Acquire a piece of ore: trace or chunk.
2. Go to the Iron Works and have a smith refine the pieces into ingots by using the BUY command.
3. Take the refined ingots to the desired store, weapon or armor, and purchase the item.

Tips for finding ore and ingots is as follows:
Levels 40-69: Orinsteel - Cave and Lake Walkers
Levels 70-100: Platinum - Basin Worms and Ragweed Beasts (best drop rate)
Levels 100: Titanium - Hydras
Levels 100+: Karnite - Dead Oak
Levels 100+: Miltanium - Completing the quest Protect Tralisia: Destroy the Dead Oak Terror Knight and fighting frostbit trolls, ice spiders, and frost wyverns
Levels 100+: Wind-metal and Lightning-metal - Tower of Windis and Lita and the Hall of Elements in the Merchant Distrct
Levels 100+: Frost-metal - Ice Prison and the Hall of Elements in the Merchant Distrct 

The city of Tralisia is always looking to improve their supply and processing of ore and
events occur to do just this so stay tuned!
