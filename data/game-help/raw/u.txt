Type: Command

Syntax: unload - unloads the equipped firearm/crossbow with its bolt/magazine.
Syntax: unload <item> - unloads the indicated firearm/crossbow with its bolt/magazine. Your equipment will be checked first, then your inventory

UNLOCK
Author: Command Master
Keywords: unlock lockpick lock pick
Type: Command

Syntax: unlock <item>|<slot> [room|inv|eq]
Using 'room', 'inv', or 'eq' will ensure an item will only be from that location (the room or your inventory or eq). Optional.


The unlock command attempts to unlock a container(chest, box, etc) or portal(gate, door,
etc). Some items require keys to be unlocked, others do not.

For containers, there are three ways to unlock it:
using the key (if one exists
asking your neighborhood Rogue to pick the lock
visiting Hoss at his Locksmith Shop in the alley behind the southern section of Merchant Distirct

For portals, there are two ways to unlock it:
using the key (if one exists
asking your neighborhood Rogue to pick the lock

Not all locks can be picked.

For unlocking affects on magic items, see help detectmagic

See: help locksmith, help lock, help open, help close, help picklocks, help detectmagic

USE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: use 
Type: Command

Syntax: use <skill name> <target> - uses the skill on the target
Leaving <target> blank will target the caster automatically.


The use command uses a skill.

Skills typically use stamina. Stamina is restored every 20 seconds out of combat. Sleeping
is the best way to restore stamina outside of restoration abilities.

Skills are unique, and the game will search for the skill to execute on every input. For


example, if 'kick' was typed, the game would first search for a regular kick command, and
if it did not find one, it would try to execute 'use kick' automatically.

To learn more about abilities please visit this web page:
http://tempestseason.com/manual/abilities/

USILIN
Type: Race
Keywords: Usilin
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Bard, Thief, Rune Mage, Psion
Racial Tensions: They form their own opinions of each race as they play
Racial Abilities: None

The Usilin, also known as the Usil Elves, are widely regarded as the most intelligent race
in Eihydia. Devoted to scholastic pursuits and magical mastery, the Usilin prioritize the
accumulation of knowledge and self-improvement as central cultural values. They distinguish
themselves from other elven races through their unwavering focus on arcane research,
intellectual discourse, and the preservation of ancient lore.

Slender in build with pale complexions and refined elven features, the Usilin carry
themselves with a quiet confidence born of deep learning. Their society revolves around the
renowned Usilin Academy, a center of education and culture where the brightest minds gather
to study the arcane arts. While their exceptional intelligence and natural magical aptitude
make them formidable spellcasters, the Usilin are physically weaker than most races and
possess limited recovery capabilities.

The Usilin follow entropic alignment tendencies and practice diverse religious traditions,
with worship spanning deities such as Taath, Rinanni, Set, Sa, and Elindale. Common
professions among the Usilin include Cleric and Runemage, though some pursue the path of
the Bard. Fighters, Thieves, and Psions are rare among their kind.

To learn more about the Usilin please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-usilin.html
