UNLOCK
Author: Command Master
Keywords: unlock lockpick lock pick
Type: Command

Syntax: unlock <item>|<slot> [room|inv|eq]
Using 'room', 'inv', or 'eq' will ensure an item will only be from that location (the room or your inventory or eq). Optional.


The unlock command attempts to unlock a container(chest, box, etc) or portal(gate, door,
etc). Some items require keys to be unlocked, others do not.

For containers, there are three ways to unlock it:
using the key (if one exists
asking your neighborhood Rogue to pick the lock
visiting Hoss at his Locksmith Shop in the alley behind the southern section of Merchant Distirct

For portals, there are two ways to unlock it:
using the key (if one exists
asking your neighborhood Rogue to pick the lock

Not all locks can be picked.

For unlocking affects on magic items, see help detectmagic

See: help locksmith, help lock, help open, help close, help picklocks, help detectmagic

CALL
Author: Command Master
Keywords: call pets animal tag mounts
Type: Command

Syntax: call <item> - summons the pet on a pet tag or mount on a mount tag!
Syntax: call <slot> - summons the pet on a pet tag or mount on a mount tag!
Syntax: call patreonPet - summons the patreon pet, if one is made. Use the Patreon command to create a pet!
Syntax: call patreonMount - summons and mounts the patreon mount, if one is made. Use the Patreon command to create a mount! (Tier 5)

Syntax: call dismiss [longID]|all - dismisses your current pet. If the tag was a single use, this pet will be lost forever.

The call command summons a mount, a new pet, dismisses a pet, or returns a lost pet to your
side.

Typically, pets and mounts must be purchased or acquired through a role play event. They
can then be summoned with the call command and controlled with the order command.

Pets can perish and be lost forever. Be careful of giving them rare and expensive items as
they can be easily lost as pet inventory is not saved. Mounts do not perish and last
forever.

POSE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: pose 
Type: Command

Syntax: pose set <string> - switches to a new pose. (Emote or Smote method)
Syntax: pose current - views the current pose.
Syntax: pose clear - resets your current pose to be default (nothing).


Pose allows a player to change their long description in the rooms they are in.

Instead of seeing the default messages:
Dayrinni the Human is here.

A custom message can be displayed, such as:
Dayrinni is here, working on something.


The pose command can also be used in a SMOTE version, by including your exact name in the
string. For example:
pose set The area glows green as you see Dayrinni praying to his god here.
This would then show up as the following in the room:
The area glows green as you see Dayrinni praying to his god here.

You can also use $n and $c for auto replacement for your name. $n will not capitalize your
name, $c will. Note: All player names are already capitalized.

This is a role play command - use it to enhance your character!


This command will be monitored for abuse. Report anything inappropriate, including OOC
messages or anything that violates the game policy.

See: help emote, help smote, help wmote, help socials, help mood, help think, help role
play

PROMPT
Author: Command Master
Keywords: prompt 
Type: Command

Syntax: prompt show          - show your current prompt format
Syntax: prompt default       - reset the prompt to the default
Syntax: prompt tokens        - list all available prompt tokens
Syntax: prompt set <format>  - set a custom format using text and/or tokens

Example: prompt set H: %h/%H S: %s/%S


The prompt command lets you customize the text displayed after every command. The format
usually shows vitals such as health and stamina.

Tokens let you embed live values in your prompt. Use % for single-char tokens (e.g. %h for
health) and {} for named tokens (e.g. {cosmic}). Type 'prompt tokens' for the full list.

Example: prompt set H: %h/%H S: %s/%S
  Displays: H: 100/100 S: 50/50

PENTHANIAN
Type: Race
Keywords: Penthanian
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Thief, Rune Mage, Psion
Racial Tensions: Frontacian, Arachnian
Racial Abilities: None

Penthanians were once part of the Frontacian Empire on the northern edge of the Great
Divide. During the Great Change, they released an ancient spell against the Arachnians that
backfired catastrophically, converting their bodies into soft crystalline substance. They
now call themselves the "Living Jewels" in their own tongue. Aloof and calculating,
Penthanians harbor a deep envy toward the Frontacians who retained their flesh and magical
jewels. They possess natural night vision and exceptional intelligence, though their
crystalline composition impedes natural healing.

To learn more about the Penthanian please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-penthanian.html

PSYCIAN
Type: Race
Keywords: Psycian
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Bard, Barbarian, Forester, Rune Mage, Psion
Racial Tensions: Frontacian
Racial Abilities: None

Psycians are graceful, human-like beings with immense telepathic power, considered the
wisest of all races. They display a calm, distant exterior that masks deep emotion and
possess natural psychic abilities allowing them to probe minds at close range. A unique
glow forms around the head when their psychic abilities are in use.

Descended from survivors of the Isle of Psycia, which was destroyed by the ancient
Frontacians roughly a thousand years ago, the Psycians originally served as a telepathic
communication network for the Frontacian Empire. They staged a covert rebellion by
spreading misinformation, and in retaliation, Frontacian mages destroyed their island and


hunted the survivors. A few Psycians escaped to rebuild in secret, and they maintain an
unbridled hatred for the Frontacians and seek to restore their lost homeland.

Psycians are calm, logical, patient, and loyal, preferring peaceful resolutions to
conflict. However, overexposure to uncontrolled minds can cause madness, requiring regular
doses of a madness cure. Near Frontacians, they display open loathing and a loss of their
characteristic restraint.

To learn more about the Psycian please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-psycian.html


See: help <ability name>, help <class name>, help races

MATERIALS
Type: Materials
Keywords: Materials,Orinsteel,Platinum,Titanium,Karnite,Miltanium,Metal,Iron Works,Forge,Ore,Ingot,Smelt

A Tempest Season there are several types of rare materials used for high leveled weapons,
armor and shields.

These materials are required to purchase gear upgrades in the Tralisia Merchant District.

1. Acquire a piece of ore: trace or chunk.
2. Go to the Iron Works and have a smith refine the pieces into ingots by using the BUY command.
3. Take the refined ingots to the desired store, weapon or armor, and purchase the item.

Tips for finding ore and ingots is as follows:
Levels 40-69: Orinsteel - Cave and Lake Walkers
Levels 70-100: Platinum - Basin Worms and Ragweed Beasts (best drop rate)
Levels 100: Titanium - Hydras
Levels 100+: Karnite - Dead Oak
Levels 100+: Miltanium - Completing the quest Protect Tralisia: Destroy the Dead Oak Terror Knight and fighting frostbit trolls, ice spiders, and frost wyverns
Levels 100+: Wind-metal and Lightning-metal - Tower of Windis and Lita and the Hall of Elements in the Merchant Distrct
Levels 100+: Frost-metal - Ice Prison and the Hall of Elements in the Merchant Distrct 

The city of Tralisia is always looking to improve their supply and processing of ore and
events occur to do just this so stay tuned!

The following materials and their respective tiers are as follows:

 -  copper (tier 2)

Higher tiers mean stronger damage and protection.
