DIE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: die revive death dying debt resurrect
Type: Command


The die command brings you back to life!

If you have been killed in combat, you may die and return to life. You will be revived at
your religion's temple, or the center of Spur if there is no temple.

Note: If Quest Mode is on, your revive location is the current room.


There are penalties associated with dying if you are over level 10: a debt to your
experience will incur. This debt must be paid back before any progress will resume for the
next level. The current debt can be seen under the score command.

You keep all of your equipment, items, and currency when you die.

(Exploring the game while under level 10 is a good idea since there are no penalties when
dying.)

See: help class ats.commands.informative.ScoreCommand, help experience, help quest mode

RENAMEITEM
Author: Command Master
Keywords: renameitem 
Type: Command

Syntax: renameitem item <item>|<slot> <new short desc> - sets the short description of the intended item.


The Rename Item command renames an item's short description to be different (and long
description automatically). The text is colored automatically.

Currently, only Dragon claw-guards can be renamed at the Dragon Sanctuary at the claw-guard
smith.

The item must be in the inventory and not equipped.

It is recommended, but not required, to retain the same material level of an item. Do not
increase the material level. Generally speaking, the renamed item should be similar in
potency and spirit.

Example: some steel claw-guards -> some bright red flaming steel claw-guards

This command is logged and reviewed, and any abuse or inappropriate text will be severely
punished including revoking usage of this command and removal of the renamed item.

TRAIN
Author: Command Master
Keywords: train trainer guildlist classlist raise
Type: Command


Syntax: train list - lists the abilities and specializations that can be trained at this location.
Syntax: train <slot> - trains the ability up to the next level. This will require a tier point and sometimes an item (the train will inform you on what is needed).
Syntax: train spec <slot> - trains the specialization up to the next level. This may require a tier point and a small fee.

Syntax: train tier cost - checks the cost of the next tier point you may buy.
Syntax: train tier buy - buys a tier point for the associated gold price.
Syntax: train tier respec confirm - resets all of your tier points for 5,000 gold. All of the abilities and specializations you trained will be set to lv 1. You will receive a 75% refund of your ability points.

Syntax: train guildlist - views your standing in your classes guild.
Syntax: train history - views your training attribute gain history.


The train command raises a level of an ability, specialization, or aptitude to the next
level.

The cost of raising to the next level varies:
1. Possibly using a TIER POINT and/or money, if applicable to raise an ability in level.
2. Using a TIER point (and money, if applicable) to raise a specialization in level.

The money cost to raise an ability up a level depends on the amount of uses the ability has
left.

You gain tier points upon leveling up.

You can also buy tier points at the trainer using 'train tier buy'.

Respeccing your tier points will set all of your non-natural abilities to their initial
level.

To learn more about training and learning new abilities visit the manual:
www.tempestseason.com/manual/abilities/#Training_Abilities_and_Specializations

See: help learn, help class ats.commands.PracticeCommand, help class
ats.commands.informative.SpecializationsCommand, help tier points, help anima convergence

RANK
Author: Command Master
Keywords: rank 
Type: Command

Syntax: rank list - lists currently known ranks.
Syntax: rank set <slot> - sets your current rank. Use rank list to get the # 
Syntax: rank clear - clears your current rank.
Syntax: rank current - views your current rank.
Syntax: rank view <rank> - views the indicated rank for more information.


The rank command controls what is seen in front of your name. Ranks are similiar to titles,
but are means of formal address, such as Sir or Lady.

To be considered ready for a rank, a player must have a clear concept of their character,
and invest the time, effort and consistency of RP needed to have a well-established IC
role. A rank can then be granted as an RP reward at the staff's discretion.

The Master rank is the only non-RP rank. It is awarded for being in 1st position on the
Guild List at the training hall.
