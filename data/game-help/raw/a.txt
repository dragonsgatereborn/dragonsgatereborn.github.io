FIRST AID
Type: Skill
Keywords: First Aid firstaid
Character Level Required: All Classes(1). 
Maximum Training Level: 1. Range: 10ft

Syntax: use firstaid <target> - applies first aid to heal a target's wounds

First Aid.

First Aid (single victim): applies first aid to heal wounds (+chance, +potency, -delay).

The target must be sitting or lying down to receive first aid. Healing scales from First
Aid level, Wisdom, and Dexterity. Result quality ranges from Partial to Critical, with a
hard cap of 35 on critical heals.

TRAINING KEY: potency - damage/effectiveness, chance - chance of failure/success, delay -
length of ability delay on use

ADVANCE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: advance 
Type: Command

Syntax: advance [units] - advances forwards in a room at max units unless specified. 1 unit = 5 feet.
Syntax: advance <target> - advances forwards to the target.

The advance command allows you to move forward in a room by a number of units. Engaged NPCs
will try to ensure they are always in attack range. If using a ranged weapon, it is best to
advance or retreat prior to starting combat. You may see the current location of all NPCs
and players in a room by using the assess command.

Author: Command Master
Keywords: call pets animal tag mounts
Type: Command

Syntax: call <item> - summons the pet on a pet tag or mount on a mount tag!
Syntax: call <slot> - summons the pet on a pet tag or mount on a mount tag!
Syntax: call patreonPet - summons the patreon pet, if one is made. Use the Patreon command to create a pet!

The color command can change many aspects of color in A Tempest Season.

#x: terminates color and reverts to client's default

#e1 to #e255: a specific color, that matches the chart from the following website:

ANTHIAN
Type: Race
Keywords: Anthian
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Bard, Thief, Barbarian, Forester, Rune Mage
Racial Tensions: They form their own opinions of each race as they play
Racial Abilities: None

Anthians are short humanoids with bird and reptilian-like features, distinguished by their
feathered wings which grant them the gift of natural flight. Males range from 4'0" to 5'2"
in height, while females span 3'7" to 4'8". They possess sharp facial characteristics and
are renowned for their exceptionally beautiful singing voices, which resemble melodic bird
chirping.

Their lightweight physiology, while enabling flight, restricts their cargo capacity and
physical strength compared to larger races. Anthians embrace a reclusive lifestyle,
establishing small communities in natural terrain rather than urban centers. They gravitate
toward canyons, bluffs and cliffs, crags and rocky outcroppings. They share cultural
parallels with Leuians, prioritizing family bonds, community loyalty, and heritage pride.

Bards represent their most frequent urban role, leveraging their vocal talents. Foresters
also align well with their nature-oriented culture. Anthians traditionally favor Elindale
as their primary deity but may follow any religious path and alignment.

To learn more about the Anthian please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-anthian.html

ARACHNIAN
Type: Race
Keywords: Arachnian
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Thief, Barbarian
Racial Tensions: Penthanian, Secian
Racial Abilities: None

Arachnians are highly intelligent spider-like beings who inhabit the Great Waste in
northern Spur's continent, ruled by the fearsome Matriarch. An ancient conflict with the
Penthanians resulted in the loss of their rune-magick abilities when Penthanian mages
unleashed UnMagick, transforming the Arachnians into chaotic, hostile beings. They are
cruel and aggressive, displaying single-mindedly biased attitudes toward other races,
particularly despising Penthanians and viewing Secians as embodying their lost magical
abilities. Some legends suggest they were originally followers of Order, and prophecies
foretell the restoration of their lost powers. Arachnians possess natural body armor in the
form of a tough carapace, night vision, high resistance to destructive magic, exceptional
strength and dexterity, and the ability to throw webs and deliver fanged bites. They are
known for their advanced understanding of alchemy theory.

To learn more about the Arachnian please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-arachnian.html
