INVENTORY
Author: Command Master
Keywords: inventory carry items
Type: Command

Syntax: inventory - displays your inventory.
Syntax: inventory sort inv|bank|room|<container name> <sort type>|list - changes the sorting method of the selected inventory.
  Use 'list' to see a list of available options.
Syntax: inventory carry - displays your carry information regarding your inventory.
Syntax: inventory updateKeys|updateGems|updateCoins - looks through all of your inventory items (not in containers) and updates all non-keys/etc to be keys, which are keys and are also non-keys during the key updating process.


The inventory command shows all currently held items.

When you receive an item, such as picking one up, it goes into your inventory. You can only
carry a limited amount of items (35 item stacks). Buying containers (such as backpacks) and
using the bank vault can store more items.

You may drop, give, inspect, keep, retain items (and more!).

See: help drop, help give, help inspect, help keep, help retain, help equipment, help box,
help vault, help cache, help break,

INSPECT
Author: Command Master
Keywords: inspect value worth examine identify
Type: Command

Syntax: inspect <item>|<slot> [room|inv|eq] - inspects an item.
Syntax: inspect <item>|<slot> [contianer] [room|inv|eq] - inspects an item inside a container.
Using 'room', 'inv', or 'eq' will ensure an item will only be from that location (the room or your inventory or eq). Optional.


The inspect command displays detailed information about a specific item, such as weight,
key words, wear locations and so on.

In order to view magical properties on an item, an Erius' Magnificent Magnifying Glass must
be in your possession. Mages can also use Detect Magic on an item to view magical
properties.

This item can be purchased from Ally's Shop, The Adventurer's Nook, in the Spur Merchant
District.

See: help magnifying glass, help detect magic, help appraise, help haggle, help bargain,
help currency

IDENTIFY
Author: Command Master
Keywords: identify 
Type: Command

ID

Appraise a piece of armor or a weapon you are carrying or wearing

Syntax: id <item> - Appraise the named item.


Syntax: id q on <item> - Quality selector (q is shorthand, on is optional).
Syntax: id magick on <item> - Detect whether an item carries a magickal aura ('mag'/'magic' also work).
Syntax: id gem on <item> - Estimate a gem's silver value.


The identify (id) command appraises an item you are carrying or wearing, giving you
an estimated newly-forged value, weight, and condition rather than its exact stats.

Identify covers four kinds of item:
  armor  - estimates a piece of armor's cost, weight, and repair state.
  weapon - estimates a weapon's cost, weight, and condition.
  gem    - estimates a gem's silver value.
  magick - detects whether an item carries a magickal aura (also 'mag' or 'magic').

All four kinds of appraisal are available. Accuracy improves with your
appraisal skill, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Perception.

Unlike inspect, identify gives an estimated value/quality appraisal rather than the
item's exact condition.

See: help inspect, help repair,

MATERIALS


Type: Materials
Keywords: Materials,Orinsteel,Platinum,Titanium,Karnite,Miltanium,Metal,Iron Works,Forge,Ore,Ingot,Smelt

A Tempest Season there are several types of rare materials used for high leveled weapons,
armor and shields.

These materials are required to purchase gear upgrades in the Tralisia Merchant District.

1. Acquire a piece of ore: trace or chunk.
2. Go to the Iron Works and have a smith refine the pieces into ingots by using the BUY command.
3. Take the refined ingots to the desired store, weapon or armor, and purchase the item.

Tips for finding ore and ingots is as follows:
Levels 40-69: Orinsteel - Cave and Lake Walkers
Levels 70-100: Platinum - Basin Worms and Ragweed Beasts (best drop rate)
Levels 100: Titanium - Hydras
Levels 100+: Karnite - Dead Oak
Levels 100+: Miltanium - Completing the quest Protect Tralisia: Destroy the Dead Oak Terror Knight and fighting frostbit trolls, ice spiders, and frost wyverns
Levels 100+: Wind-metal and Lightning-metal - Tower of Windis and Lita and the Hall of Elements in the Merchant Distrct
Levels 100+: Frost-metal - Ice Prison and the Hall of Elements in the Merchant Distrct 

The city of Tralisia is always looking to improve their supply and processing of ore and
events occur to do just this so stay tuned!
