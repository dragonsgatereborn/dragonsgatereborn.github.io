Frontacians are one of the oldest and most influential races in Aradath, having ruled the
greatest single Empire the world has ever seen. During the Second and Third Ages they built

fragile compared to martial races, they compensate with unmatched intellect, cunning, and
raw magical power. Frontacians exhibit considerable imperial pride and a cold, uncaring
demeanor toward those they consider lesser, though honesty holds significant cultural value
within their own society.
