Thugians are massive, brutish humanoids renowned for their exceptional physical power and
remarkable ability to survive. Considered among the strongest of all humanoid races, these
towering beings combine tremendous muscle with an almost supernatural capacity for rapid
healing, recovering from wounds that would fell lesser creatures. Their intelligence,
however, is severely limited, and their communication skills are poor at best.

Despite their fearsome reputation, Thugians have simple needs. They are almost constantly
hungry and will consume whatever is readily available, including the flesh of other
sentient beings, with particular fondness for humans and trolls. They are easily
entertained and attracted to small, shiny, or cute objects, becoming fiercely possessive of
anything that catches their eye. A well-fed Thugian can become a surprisingly loyal
companion, though their chaotic nature and unpredictable temperament make their anger
difficult to foresee. Preferred professions include Fighter and Barbarian, while Thief and
Rune Mage are exceedingly rare among their kind.
