Create an account from the login screen

Registration is self-service and does not require an email address.

  1. Connect to game.dragonsgatereborn.com on port 8555.
  2. Choose 2. Create new account on the login screen.
  3. Choose an account name that is different from your character name, then create and confirm your password.
  4. From the account menu, choose C. Create new character.

Live connection and account creation verified August 9, 2026. Never post or share your password.
