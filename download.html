Game Client (Future Feature Release)

Our dedicated game-client package is being prepared for a future feature release.

In the meantime, these are strong client options for connecting to MUDs:

  • Mudlet - Free, open source, and cross-platform (Windows/macOS/Linux). Great for scripting and mapping.
  • MUSHclient - Classic and lightweight Windows client with strong trigger/alias/plugin support.
  • TinTin++ - Powerful terminal client with advanced scripting and automation.
  • CMUD - Feature-rich Windows client from the zMUD/CMUD toolchain.

Use the connection info from the Play page: How to Play.
