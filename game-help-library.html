Anthians are short humanoids with bird and reptilian-like features, distinguished by their
feathered wings which grant them the gift of natural flight. Males range from 4'0" to 5'2"
in height, while females span 3'7" to 4'8". They possess sharp facial characteristics and
are renowned for their exceptionally beautiful singing voices, which resemble melodic bird
chirping.

Their lightweight physiology, while enabling flight, restricts their cargo capacity and
physical strength compared to larger races. Anthians embrace a reclusive lifestyle,
establishing small communities in natural terrain rather than urban centers. They gravitate
toward canyons, bluffs and cliffs, crags and rocky outcroppings. They share cultural
parallels with Leuians, prioritizing family bonds, community loyalty, and heritage pride.

Bards represent their most frequent urban role, leveraging their vocal talents. Foresters
also align well with their nature-oriented culture. Anthians traditionally favor Elindale
as their primary deity but may follow any religious path and alignment.

The color command can change many aspects of color in A Tempest Season.

The following color codes exist:
#c/#C: cyan/bright cyan
#r/#R: red/bright red
#g/#G: green/bright green
#b/#B: blue/bright blue
#p/#P: purple/bright purple
#y/#Y: yellow/bright yellow
#s/#S: gray/black
#w/#W: gray/white
#d: default color
#h: default highlight color
#x: terminates color and reverts to client's default


The game also supports xterm color (type: color xterm to turn on):
#e1 to #e255: a specific color, that matches the chart from the following website:
https://i.stack.imgur.com/KTSQa.png

The game also supports true color (type: color trueColor to turn on):
#t(6-digit hex code): a specific color that matches the hex code in rgb color space. Example: #t(e3cdb6)
#t[keyword]: a specific color that matches the keyword. Example: #t[cotton]
 Note: Keywords are added by Actinate and Dayrinni. Not all keywords will exist.

See: help put, help get, help look,

The delay command shows the current delay and combat timer of a player.

5. Type look <name>

6. Type desc
7. Type @c (to clear all the text)
8. Go to Step 2.

Type: Command


The die command brings you back to life!

If you have been killed in combat, you may die and return to life. You will be revived at
your religion's temple, or the center of Spur if there is no temple.

Note: If Quest Mode is on, your revive location is the current room.


There are penalties associated with dying if you are over level 10: a debt to your

next level. The current debt can be seen under the score command.

You keep all of your equipment, items, and currency when you die.

(Exploring the game while under level 10 is a good idea since there are no penalties when
dying.)

EMOTE Command
EMOTE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: emote 
Type: Command

Syntax: emote <message> - displays the message to the room, with your name first.


Emote displays a message to the room for role play purposes. Your name is highlighted in
yellow and quoted speech is highlighted in green. You can use $c to insert your capitalized
name.

Example:
emote jumps up on the table
Would show up as:
Dayrinni jumps up on the table.

A message that opens with punctuation runs straight on from your name, so possessives and
asides read naturally. A message that does not already end in . ? or ! gets a period.
Example:
emote 's eyes narrow
Would show up as:
Dayrinni's eyes narrow.

You can insert your character's pronouns based on your sex: $e (he/she), $s (his/her), and
$m (him/her).
Example:
emote grins and cracks $s knuckles.
Would show up as (for a male character):
Dayrinni grins and cracks his knuckles.

Capitalized $E (he/she), $S (his/her), and $M (him/her) insert your target's pronouns
instead - the nearest player or NPC you name with @ before the token.
Example:
emote claps @john on the back as $E grins.
Would show up as (if John is male):
Dayrinni claps John on the back as he grins.

You can target players, NPCs, or objects using the @ prefix, which is replaced with the
target's name.
Example:
emote looks at @john and waves at @sword
Would show up as:


Dayrinni looks at John and waves at an iron sword.

Targets are searched in this order: players/NPCs in the room, inventory, equipment, then
room objects.

Punctuation after a target is kept, so you can write possessives and asides straight
through.
Example:
emote looks at @john's red eyes, then at @sword...
Would show up as:
Dayrinni looks at John's red eyes, then at an iron sword...

For precise targeting, use a prefix modifier:
@name - searches all (player/NPC, then inventory, equipment, room)
@self or @me - your own name, so a line can refer back to you
@p:name - player/NPC only
@i:name - inventory only (also supports slot: @i:2 for slot 2)
@e:name - equipment only
@r:name - room only (also supports slot: @r:3 for slot 3)
