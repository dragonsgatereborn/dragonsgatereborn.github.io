The Geography of Dragon's Gate

Overview

Aradath sits within a vast crater stretched north to south. At its lowest point lies Crescent Lake, with plateaus and islands rising from the basin. Many cultures settled on these plateaus and islands to keep space between rival peoples.

Map links will be added here as the atlas is updated.

Spur

  • The greatest city in the known civilized world is the Spur. It resides on a large central continent (called the continent of Aradath) in the Crescent Lake. There are larger cities than the Spur, but none so often visited. The Spur is so named because the other continents and islands surrounding it are as the spokes and rim of a wheel, with the Spur at the center.

More on Spur can be found in the Library section.

Rathan

  • Founded in the year 237 by the revolutionist, Rathanus Stalberdin, the city of Rathan is world famous for its impenetrable defenses and vast silver and moonsilver. Built into the mountainside of the Silver Mountains in the Great Divide, Rathan stands guard to the pass through the Divide and provides a shining example for the rest of the world of how harmony can be achieved within a city of many religions and races.

More on Rathan can be found in the Library section.

Emjedah

  • Emjedah is a coastal city to the northeast of Treehaven and southeast from Rathan. The city was first settled by travelers looking for new adventures and possibly any riches to be found.
