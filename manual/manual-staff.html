Elders and Guides

Elders

Elders (Game Masters) are caretakers and lorekeepers of the world. They build, repair, write, moderate, and guide the living story. Their priority is keeping the community healthy and the world running smoothly.

Getting Help

Guides are experienced players who help Elders support the community. They can answer questions about commands, systems, and getting started.

Elders and Guides are not a replacement for the manual or the Training Grounds, but they’re here when you’re stuck or need a human answer.

Use the HELP command to explore command categories and details. HELP shows categories, HELP <category> lists commands, and HELP <command> explains a specific command.

The Assist Command

If something breaks or you encounter an issue that can’t be resolved through role‑play, use ASSIST to request help. You only need to send it once. Your request stays active until resolved or you use ASSIST CANCEL.

If an Elder asks for details, use REPORT to send a quick summary. You can also ask fellow adventurers—this is a social world, and players often help each other out.
