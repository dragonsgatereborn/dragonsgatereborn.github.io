Character Identity

Build a character with a clear voice, believable motivations, and ties to the world. This chapter pairs well with the Races, Classes, and Characteristics sections.

  • Choose a background that explains why your character is adventuring.
  • Define a voice, temperament, and a few consistent traits.
  • Anchor your story to a region, faith, or organization.
  • Set short‑term goals to guide ongoing role‑play.
