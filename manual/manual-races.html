Choose your heritage

Dragon's Gate Reborn offers twenty playable races, each with unique characteristics, abilities, and cultural backgrounds. Your choice of race shapes your character's journey through Aradath, influencing your interactions, strengths, and role-play opportunities.

Restricted and legacy races are documented separately for lore and planning. Their presence in this guide does not confirm current character-creation access.

Race Selection Tips

Consider these factors when choosing your race:

  • Race affects your base characteristics and abilities
  • Some races have unique role-play requirements and restrictions
  • Certain classes may be better suited to specific races
  • Your race influences how NPCs and other players perceive you
  • Dragons progress through distinct life stages as they advance
  • Each race has its own cultural traditions and gathering places
