In-Game Help Text

GO-BLIN
Type: Race
Keywords: Go-blin
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Bard, Thief, Barbarian, Forester, Rune Mage, Psion
Racial Tensions: They form their own opinions of each race as they play
Racial Abilities: None

Go-blins are small, wretched creatures that dwell in dark cave systems, organized into
tight-knit clans. Standing only three to four feet tall, they possess a distinctive warty
green hide covered in pulsating yellow warts, broken tusks, and filthy skin. Their
uniformly black eyes peer out from faces universally considered repulsive, featuring
mottled green or grey skin with various visible deformities.

These nocturnal beings are compulsive hoarders, accumulating refuse and items of
questionable value from raids and scavenging expeditions. Spitting serves as a common
greeting among their kind, and they actively avoid sunlight whenever possible. Go-blins are
punks and bullies by nature, sadistic when the balance is in their favor, yet cowardly when
facing genuine threats. They prey on weaker creatures but scatter when confronted by
superior force, valuing cowardice as a survival mechanism and disdaining those who stand
and fight needlessly.

To learn more about the Go-blin please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-goblin.html
