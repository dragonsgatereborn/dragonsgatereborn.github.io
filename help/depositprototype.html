DEPOSIT

Deposit materials and gold into the material bin (Barrin's Bountiful Bin)


Syntax: deposit store [item name|slot] - Deposit an item in the material bin. You won't be able to retrieve it after.
Syntax: deposit storestack [item name|slot] - Deposit an entire stack of items into the material bin.
Syntax: deposit gold [amount] - Deposit gold into the material bin.
Syntax: deposit silver [amount] - Deposit silver into the material bin (converted to gold, 100 silver = 1 gold).
Syntax: deposit view [players|item nid|player name] - View the bin; 'players' shows each player's contribution %, or (staff/helper) pass an item nid or a player name.


The deposit command allows you to deposit materials and gold into the material bin
(Barrin's Bountiful Bin) for the city. Spur tracks everything that is deposited - both
current and all time. The more materials added to the bin the more the city can build
structures with. Some materials can be enchanted - see the help topic below.

Please be aware that any items deposited will be removed from your inventory and there is
NO guarantee if you will receive reimbursement from this. City officials will remove the
materials for use in the city and they may directly or indirectly reward the players.

See: help inventory, help inspect, help infuse material
