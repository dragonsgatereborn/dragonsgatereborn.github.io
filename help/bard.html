In-Game Help Text

BARD
Type: Class
Keywords: Bard
Difficulty: MEDIUM
Role: Utility, Control, Healer
Allowed Races: Anthian, Drag-al, Fir Elf, Flerian, Frontacian, Go-blin, Hithual, Human,
Psycian, San Elf, Usilin
Specializations: None

Bards are versatile entertainers capable of casting spells and combat, though their magical
abilities are less powerful than dedicated spell-casters. They gain some thief-like
abilities including disguise and excel at performance-based roleplay. The class suits
players who value versatility, charm, and creative problem-solving within group dynamics.


To learn more about the Bard please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/classes/class-bard.html
