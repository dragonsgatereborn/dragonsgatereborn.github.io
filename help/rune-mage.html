In-Game Help Text

RUNE MAGE
Type: Class
Keywords: Rune Mage
Difficulty: MEDIUM
Role: Damage, Control
Allowed Races: Anthian, Drag-al, Flerian, Frontacian, Go-blin, Go-blin-al, Human, Leuian,
Monitanian, Muatana-al, Penthanian, Psycian, San Elf, Secian, Thugian, Usilin
Specializations: None

Rune Mages achieve their power through scholarly discipline and study rather than divine
favor. They excel in both offensive and defensive magic, capable of reshaping battlefields
with area spells despite being physically weaker than martial classes.


To learn more about the Rune Mage please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/classes/class-runemage.html
