PSION
Type: Class
Keywords: Psion
Difficulty: MEDIUM
Role: Control, Damage, Debuffer
Allowed Races: Flerian, Go-blin, Hithual, Human, Leuian, Penthanian, Psycian, San Elf,
Secian, Usilin
Specializations: None

Psions wield psionics: the ability to use only one's mind to affect the world around them.
They tend to be deep thinkers who operate under common-sense methodologies, often pursuing
enlightenment through mental perfection rather than religious devotion.


To learn more about the Psion please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/classes/class-psion.html
