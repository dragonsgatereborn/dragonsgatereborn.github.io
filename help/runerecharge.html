RUNERECHARGE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: runerecharge
Type: Command

Syntax: recharge list - displays your current rune weaves and availability.
Syntax: recharge all - recharges (renews) all available runes to their maximum weaves.
Syntax: recharge <name> - recharges (renews) a specific rune type.


The recharge command restores (renews) your rune weaves at a rune vendor. Only rune types used by spells you are trained enough to cast can be recharged.
