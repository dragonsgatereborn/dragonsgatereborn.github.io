In-Game Help Text

DROP
Author: Command Master


Keywords: drop
Type: Command

Syntax: drop <item> - drops the item stack from your inventory.
Syntax: drop <item> - drops the of the item stack. IE: drop 2.sword would drop the item stack that contains the 2nd sword.
Syntax: drop <slot> - drops the item stack at the slot. Use inventory to find the slot number.
Syntax: drop # <item>|<item>|<slot> - drops number of items from your inventory.

Syntax: drop all [item] - drops everything, or all items with same name as input, from your inventory.

Syntax: drop <amount> gold - drops the indicated gold.
Syntax: drop <amount> silver - drops the indicated silver.
Syntax: drop <amount> gold <amount> silver - drops the indicated gold and silver.


The drop command allows you to place an item from your inventory into the room.
