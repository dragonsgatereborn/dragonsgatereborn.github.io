In-Game Help Text

THINK


Author: Command Master
Keywords: think
Type: Command

Syntax: think <message> - sends an in character think message to all staff members.
This is great for RP as it assists the staff in moving events along for your character!

The think command allows your character to have a private thought, which is sent to all
Staff members.

Thinking during any role play is vital (and highly encouraged) to your characters
development because it allows the Staff to be notified of active RP and also non-obvious
information.

Knowing what a character is thinking can allow the staff to move the quest into a more
meaningful direction. For example: a character may be willing to con a NPC but aren't sure
if it is possible. A thought about this can give the Staff the information they need to
open up this line of RP!

Periodically, bonus experience and other RP rewards are given out to characters who use the
think command often.

Note: Other characters, such as those with psionics, are able to hear thoughts.

This is a role play command - use it to enhance your character!

See: help feel, help socials, help emote, help smote, help pose, help wmote, help mood,
help role play
