In-Game Help Text

SECIAN
Type: Race
Keywords: Secian
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric: Order of
the Hand, Rune Mage, Psion
Racial Tensions: Arachnian
Racial Abilities: None

Beautiful winged creatures, the Secians are the tiniest of all the races of Aradath. These
fairy-like beings hail from the ancient city-state of Secia, transformed during the Great
Change. Known for their ruby-red eyes and dazzling blonde hair, Secians are consistently
cheerful and compassionate, possessing empathy as a divine gift. They are unfailingly kind,
serving as peacemakers who reject violence and avoid lawbreaking or deception. Secians are
capable of flight with minimal exertion and possess exceptional healing powers through
empathic connection. They maintain strong, ancient bonds with Dragons and have a well-known
obsession with jewels and gemstones. Most commonly found as Heart Clerics and Rune-Mages,
Secians are extremely fragile but compensate with rapid movement and recovery.

To learn more about the Secian please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-secian.html
