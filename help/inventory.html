INVENTORY
Author: Command Master
Keywords: inventory carry items
Type: Command

Syntax: inventory - displays your inventory.
Syntax: inventory sort inv|bank|room|<container name> <sort type>|list - changes the sorting method of the selected inventory.
  Use 'list' to see a list of available options.
Syntax: inventory carry - displays your carry information regarding your inventory.
Syntax: inventory updateKeys|updateGems|updateCoins - looks through all of your inventory items (not in containers) and updates all non-keys/etc to be keys, which are keys and are also non-keys during the key updating process.


The inventory command shows all currently held items.

When you receive an item, such as picking one up, it goes into your inventory. You can only
carry a limited amount of items (35 item stacks). Buying containers (such as backpacks) and
using the bank vault can store more items.

You may drop, give, inspect, keep, retain items (and more!).

See: help drop, help give, help inspect, help keep, help retain, help equipment, help box,
help vault, help cache, help break,
