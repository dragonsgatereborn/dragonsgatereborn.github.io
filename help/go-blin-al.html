In-Game Help Text

GO-BLIN-AL
Type: Race
Keywords: Go-blin-al
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Thief, Barbarian, Rune Mage
Racial Tensions: They form their own opinions of each race as they play
Racial Abilities: None

Go-blin-als are believed to be servants to Chaos, possibly created through the warped
experiments of the Chaos Lords. Scholars suggest they may be remnants of a formerly mighty


race, corrupted and twisted by dark magic into the ugly, mottled green creatures seen
today. Standing approximately three feet tall, these bipedal beings are typically filthy
and foul-tempered, dwelling in caves within tight-knit clans.

Nasty, violent, and full of mischief, Go-blin-als are bullies and punks who become sadistic
when they hold the advantage but turn cowardly when facing genuine threats. They avoid
sunlight and delight in harassing weaker creatures. Spitting at each other is a common form
of greeting among their kind. They speak with distinctive lisp-like patterns and an
underdeveloped grasp of Common.

Go-blin-als possess natural night vision, quickness, agility, and dexterity, though they
suffer from a troublemaker reputation, physical weakness, and communication barriers with
other races. Their chaotic alignment draws them primarily to the profession of Thief, with
rare individuals becoming Fighters, Barbarians, or Rune Mages.

To learn more about the Go-blin-al please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-goblinal.html
