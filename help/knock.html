In-Game Help Text

KNOCK
Author: Command Master
Keywords: knock
Type: Command

Syntax: knock <item> [room|inv|eq] - knocks on an item. If it's a common door or gate it will make a sound on the other side.
Using 'room', 'inv', or 'eq' will ensure an item will only be from that location (the room or your inventory or eq). Optional.
