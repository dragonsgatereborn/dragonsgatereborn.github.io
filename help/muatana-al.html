In-Game Help Text

MUATANA-AL
Type: Race
Keywords: Muatana-al
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Thief, Barbarian, Rune Mage
Racial Tensions: San Elf
Racial Abilities: None

The origins of the Muatana-al remain shrouded in mystery. Some believe they were once
cursed nobility, while others claim the Chaos Lords created them as instruments of terror.
Primarily associated with the Isle of Muldavia, they dominate the San Elves and their
leader, the Muatana-al Lord of the Dernath Woods, has allegedly forged pacts with Dragons
and Wyverns.

Tall and gaunt, the Muatana-al possess unnaturally pale flesh that seems to glow faintly in
darkness, with luminous veins visible beneath the surface in hues of crimson, blue, green,
and violet. They are cold, calculating, and utterly without mercy — intelligent predators
and patient hunters capable of stalking prey for extended periods. Solitary by nature, they
form alliances only when mutually beneficial, having long since abandoned any semblance of
compassion or empathy.

The Muatana-al must regularly drain the life force of living creatures to sustain
themselves. Feeding requires physical contact, leaving victims weakened, aged, or
desiccated. Without feeding, a Muatana-al suffers progressive weakness and eventual death.
They are strictly nocturnal creatures — sunlight causes searing pain and prolonged exposure
is lethal. Their entire culture revolves around the cycle of night and day.

To learn more about the Muatana-al please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-muatanaal.html
