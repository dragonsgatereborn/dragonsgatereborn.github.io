In-Game Help Text

DRAG-AL
Type: Race
Keywords: Drag-al
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Bard, Thief, Barbarian, Forester, Rune Mage
Racial Tensions: Monitanian
Racial Abilities: None

Drag-als are short, dragon-like beings who possess the power to breathe fire but lack the
claws and wings of true dragons. These jovial creatures enjoy jokes, ale, and storytelling,
naturally gravitating toward trading and performance work rather than laborious toil. Their
natural scale armor provides innate protection, and they possess night vision along with a
knowledge of additional languages. Drag-als form friendships readily and are known for
their superior bargaining skills, though their small stature creates challenges in direct
combat. Monitanians regard them negatively, viewing them as loud and obnoxious. Common
professions include Bard and Runemage, with some pursuing paths as Thieves, Fighters, or
Clerics. Barbarians and Foresters among them are rare.

To learn more about the Drag-al please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-dragal.html
