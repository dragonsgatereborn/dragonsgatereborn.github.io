TRAIN
Author: Command Master
Keywords: train trainer guildlist classlist raise
Type: Command

Syntax: train list - lists the abilities and specializations that can be trained at this location.
Syntax: train <slot> - trains the ability up to the next level. This will require a tier point and sometimes an item (the train will inform you on what is needed).
Syntax: train spec <slot> - trains the specialization up to the next level. This may require a tier point and a small fee.

Syntax: train tier cost - checks the cost of the next tier point you may buy.
Syntax: train tier buy - buys a tier point for the associated gold price.
Syntax: train tier respec confirm - resets all of your tier points for 5,000 gold. All of the abilities and specializations you trained will be set to lv 1. You will receive a 75% refund of your ability points.

Syntax: train guildlist - views your standing in your classes guild.
Syntax: train history - views your training attribute gain history.


The train command raises a level of an ability, specialization, or aptitude to the next
level.

The cost of raising to the next level varies:
1. Possibly using a TIER POINT and/or money, if applicable to raise an ability in level.
2. Using a TIER point (and money, if applicable) to raise a specialization in level.


The money cost to raise an ability up a level depends on the amount of uses the ability has
left.

You gain tier points upon leveling up.

You can also buy tier points at the trainer using 'train tier buy'.

Respeccing your tier points will set all of your non-natural abilities to their initial
level.

To learn more about training and learning new abilities visit the manual:
www.tempestseason.com/manual/abilities/#Training_Abilities_and_Specializations
