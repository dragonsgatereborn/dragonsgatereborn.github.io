In-Game Help Text

PLACE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: place
Type: Command

Syntax: place <item> - places the item from your inventory.
Syntax: place <item> - places the of the item. IE: place 2.sword would place the 2nd sword.
Syntax: place <slot> - places the item at the slot. Use inventory to find the slot number.
Syntax: place all - places everything from your inventory.
Syntax: place <amount> gold - places the indicated gold.
Syntax: place <amount> silver - places the indicated silver.
Syntax: place <amount> gold <amount> silver - places the indicated gold and silver.
You need ownership of the room in order to place items such they last long term.
Any items PUT into a PLACED container in the room will be SAVED through copyovers, etc.
