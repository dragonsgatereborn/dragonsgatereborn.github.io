REVEAL
Author: Command Master
Keywords: reveal
Type: Command

Syntax: reveal - searches the room attempting the make any hidden targets drop from the shadows.


To simply search a room, type search room


The reveal command is used to force players or npcs who are hidden in the shadows to reveal
themselves. The chance depends on the players Abstraction and Coordination attributes vs
the targets hide skill.

It is harder to reveal rather than to search.

Delay is reduced by the players hide skill and Flexibility attribute.

See: help search, help hide
