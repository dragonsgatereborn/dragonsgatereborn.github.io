In-Game Help Text

GIVE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: give
Type: Command

Syntax: give # <item>|<slot> <player> - gives the item stack from your inventory to the player.
Syntax: give <amount> gold <player> - gives the amount of gold from your inventory to the player.
Syntax: give <amount> silver <player> - gives the amount of silver from your inventory to the player.
Syntax: give <amount> gold <amount> silver <player> - gives the amount of gold and silver from your inventory to the player.


The give command allows you to transfer an item or items to another player..

To give a single item, from a stack, to a player, type: give 1 <player> <item>. Example:
give 1 sword dayrinni.
