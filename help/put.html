PUT
Author: Command Master
Keywords: put
Type: Command

Syntax: put <item>|<slot> [in] <container> [room|inv|eq] - puts an item stack from your inventory into the corpse or container.
Syntax: put # <item>|<slot> [in] <container> [room|inv|eq] - puts an item stack from your inventory into the corpse or container.


Syntax: put all [item] [in] <<container> [room|inv|eq] - puts all, or all items with same name as input, of the items from your inventory into the corpse or container.
Using 'room', 'inv', or 'eq' will ensure an item will only be from that location (the room or your inventory or eq). Optional.


The put command allows a player to place an object into another object.

Items can only be placed in a container, such as a chest or trunk.
