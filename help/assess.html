ASSESS
Author: Command Master
Keywords: assess
Type: Command

Syntax: assess - displays everyones location in the room.

The assess command gives a detailed listing of every NPC and player's location in the room
and how many units you may move at a time.

See: help advance, help retreat, help move, help fire, help ranger
