CLERIC: ORDER OF THE HEART
Type: Class
Keywords: Cleric: Order of the Heart
Difficulty: EASY
Role: Healer, Utility
Allowed Races: Anthian, Arachnian, Drag-al, Fir Elf, Flerian, Go-blin, Hithual, Human,
Leuian, Psycian, San Elf, Secian, Usilin
Specializations: None

Heart Clerics serve as primarily healers of their temples, concentrating on healing and
support rune-magick. They function as backline anchors, developing channeling expertise to
heal wounds and inflict damage through divine power.


To learn more about the Cleric: Order of the Heart please visit the web page:


http://dragonsgatereborn.com/classes/class-cleric-heart.html
