In-Game Help Text

ANALYZE
Author: Command Master
Keywords: analyze diagnose
Type: Command

Syntax: analyze - analyizes all players and npcs in the room.
Syntax: analyze <target>|self [full] - analyizes the player or npc in the room. Use the full option to show affects on the target.
Syntax: analyze <target>|self affects - analyizes the player's affects.
Syntax: analyze <target>|self myaffects - analyizes the player's affects you put on them.
Note: You may shorthand the above (full, affects, myaffects) for easier typing.
Note: You may also use 'self' to target you.


The analyze command allows a player to view important diagnostic information on a target.

This information includes condition (ie: near death), affects, and so on.

Analyze is a vital command to playing utility and healing classes such as Clerics and
Arcanists.
