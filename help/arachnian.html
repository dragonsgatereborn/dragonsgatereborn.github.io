In-Game Help Text

ARACHNIAN
Type: Race
Keywords: Arachnian
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Cleric: Order of the Heart, Cleric: Order of the Sword, Cleric:
Order of the Hand, Thief, Barbarian
Racial Tensions: Penthanian, Secian
Racial Abilities: None

Arachnians are highly intelligent spider-like beings who inhabit the Great Waste in
northern Spur's continent, ruled by the fearsome Matriarch. An ancient conflict with the
Penthanians resulted in the loss of their rune-magick abilities when Penthanian mages
unleashed UnMagick, transforming the Arachnians into chaotic, hostile beings. They are
cruel and aggressive, displaying single-mindedly biased attitudes toward other races,
particularly despising Penthanians and viewing Secians as embodying their lost magical
abilities. Some legends suggest they were originally followers of Order, and prophecies
foretell the restoration of their lost powers. Arachnians possess natural body armor in the
form of a tough carapace, night vision, high resistance to destructive magic, exceptional
strength and dexterity, and the ability to throw webs and deliver fanged bites. They are
known for their advanced understanding of alchemy theory.

To learn more about the Arachnian please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-arachnian.html
