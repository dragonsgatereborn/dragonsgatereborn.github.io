KEEP
Author: Command Master
Keywords: keep kept
Type: Command

Syntax: keep <item>|<slot> - marks/unmarks the item as kept - you won't sell or break it.
To keep a container during selling, mark it RETAIN. See help: retain.


The keep command allow you to protect an item in your inventory from selling, breaking, or
dropping.

To prevent an item from being placed into containers, flag the item retain.
