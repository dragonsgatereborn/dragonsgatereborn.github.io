In-Game Help Text

MONITANIAN
Type: Race
Keywords: Monitanian
Genders: Male, Female, Non-Binary
Allowed Classes: Fighter, Thief, Barbarian, Rune Mage
Racial Tensions: Flerian, Go-blin-al, Drag-al
Racial Abilities: None

Monitanians are large, scaled, upright-walking lizard-like beings supported by powerful
tails. Originating primarily from the Dismal Swamp, they remain mysterious about their
history, though historical records recognize them as among the world's bravest peoples.
They communicate through gestures, hisses, and grunts, and cannot speak the Common tongue
due to vocal limitations, though they are highly intelligent despite racial assumptions
otherwise.

Monitanians value honor and allegiance above all else. Friendships are forged through
battlefield experiences and endure permanently. They possess natural clawing and biting
attacks, thick scaled skin that provides inherent armor, and night vision capability. Known
for their exceptional strength and sturdy physiology, they prefer the heaviest armor
available and often become mercenaries for just causes.


To learn more about the Monitanian please visit the web page:
http://dragonsgatereborn.com/races/race-monitanian.html
