In-Game Help Text

LOCKSMITH
Author: Command Master
Keywords: locksmith
Type: Command

Syntax: locksmith <item> - asks the locksmith to unlock this item.
Syntax: locksmith all [empty] - asks the locksmith to unlock and open all your locked items. If 'empty' is used he buys the chests and gives you the items.


The locksmith command, when performed at a locksmith, unlocks all containers for a small
fee per chest.

The locksmith can be found in the Spur Merchant District: all south, through the alley, all
east, through the door.

It is better to seek out a Rogue to perform unlocking services as much more gold will be
gained!

See: help open, help unlock, help close, help lock, help rogue, help pick lock
