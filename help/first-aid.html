FIRST AID
Type: Skill
Keywords: First Aid firstaid
Character Level Required: All Classes(1).
Maximum Training Level: 1. Range: 10ft

Syntax: use firstaid <target> - applies first aid to heal a target's wounds

First Aid.

First Aid (single victim): applies first aid to heal wounds (+chance, +potency, -delay).

The target must be sitting or lying down to receive first aid. Healing scales from First
Aid level, Wisdom, and Dexterity. Result quality ranges from Partial to Critical, with a
hard cap of 35 on critical heals.

TRAINING KEY: potency - damage/effectiveness, chance - chance of failure/success, delay -
length of ability delay on use
