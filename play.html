New Player Guide

Your first 15 minutes

Choose a client, connect to the world, create your character, and learn the commands that will get you moving.

Connection details Starter commands

Step 1

Choose a game client

Dragon's Gate Reborn is a text-based online world. You connect with a MUD client—the program that displays the world and sends your commands.

View free client options, including Mudlet for Windows, macOS, and Linux. If you already use a MUD client, you can enter the connection information below directly.

Step 2

Connect to Dragon's Gate Reborn

SSL/TLS connection

Host: game.dragonsgatereborn.com
Port: 9555
SSL/TLS: Enabled

Certificate notice: The server is currently presenting an expired certificate issued for Tempest Season rather than game.dragonsgatereborn.com. Some clients may refuse this connection or display a warning until the game-server certificate is renewed.

Standard connection

Host: game.dragonsgatereborn.com
Port: 8555
SSL/TLS: Disabled

Use this option if your client cannot connect through SSL/TLS.

Direct IP: 107.161.22.78. The hostname is preferred. Port 8555 is the dependable option while certificate maintenance for port 9555 is pending.

Step 3

Follow the login prompts

After connecting, follow the instructions shown on the login screen. If you need access or are unsure how to continue, ask in the community Discord rather than sharing account information publicly.

Step 4

Try these starter commands

Type a command at the prompt and press Enter. Commands are not case-sensitive.

CommandWhat it does
LOOKLook at your current room.
LOOK <thing>Examine a person, object, or feature.
NORTH, SOUTH, EAST, WESTMove through an available exit. Directions can usually be shortened to one letter.
'Hello!Speak to everyone in the room.
SMILEPerform a social action.
GET <item>Pick up an item.
INVENTORYReview what you are carrying.
STATUSReview important information about your character.
HELPShow help categories.
HELP <command>Read detailed help for a command.

For command syntax, communication, movement, and the full in-game help system, continue to Getting Around.
