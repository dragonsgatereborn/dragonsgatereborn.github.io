Overview

Psions are mental specialists who turn PSI discipline into direct combat pressure, control effects, and perception utility. The class centers on resource timing and decision quality: overcast aggressively and you risk collapse, pace well and you control fights through precision. While Psycians remain the iconic psionic race, any race with strong intelligence, willpower, and composure can build an effective psion profile. Psions are traditionally portrayed as focused thinkers who value mindcraft over deity worship.

Learning and Training

Use SKILLS PSI to review your Psionics skill. At an appropriate trainer, use LEARN LIST for new abilities and TRAIN LIST for advancement. Guild and trainer locations should be discovered in play or confirmed with the community.

Characteristic Focus

- Intelligence: Primary stat for psionic control and precision. - Willpower: Core stat for psionic endurance and resistance pressure. - Wisdom: Helpful for stable decision-making and utility use under stress. - Constitution: Important for fatigue and survival in extended encounters. - Alignment: Usually roleplayed as focused, disciplined, and mentally driven.

Elder RP Note

Psion roleplay works best when calm external behavior contrasts with high internal intensity. In practice: speak like a strategist, fight like a control specialist, and treat PSI depletion as a meaningful risk point rather than a minor inconvenience.
