Heart Cleric

A Clerical order focused on discipline, faith, and purpose.

Order Overview

Order of the Heart (Heart Clerics) are primarily healers of their temples, expected to master channeling, lay hands, and restorative spellwork. Most devote themselves to protecting life and stabilizing the wounded, though some faiths may emphasize darker rites that blur into necromancy.

Cleric Responsibilities

  • Dedication and continued contribution to a deity.
  • Upholding of the deity's standards by preaching and exhibiting the advantages, expectations, and ideals of their deity and religious code.
  • The conversion of characters (requiring such) to the deity's ways.

Signature Spells

  • Healer's Touch
  • Touch of Vigor
  • Touch of Harm
  • Light Globe
  • Dart of Darkness
  • Light Dart

Beta note: Heart Cleric is selectable now. Its spell list and balance continue to be refined through live testing.

Level 5 branches by deity restriction (Light Dart and Dart of Darkness are parallel entries).

Playstyle Tips

  • Stay near the front line but keep space to triage allies.
  • Rotate channeling and spell heals to manage fatigue.
  • Carry support utility for cleansing and light damage.
  • Pick a deity focus early to guide spell and roleplay choices.

Guild Locations

Spur In the Temples.

Special Abilities

Channeling Channeling the god-given ability to heal wounds and damage. Turning Turning is a clerical ability to immobilize, fear, or dust (kill) undead.

Characteristic Focus

- Wisdom: Very useful in both the use of healing as well as the use of magickal spells. - Dexterity/Agility: Useful in combat. - Constitution: Useful for combat survival as fatigue and hit points are modified by this score. - Strength: Very useful as this class is very good at combat - Willpower: Helpful in turning the undead. - Alignment: Dependent upon the specific deity.
