Psionics uses its own PSI resource

A newly created Human Psion currently begins with Centering, Dart, and Telepathy. Race, class, level, and future beta changes can alter the abilities shown to another character.

See what you can do

CommandWhat it shows
INFO PSIAvailable psionic abilities, their short names, and abilities still locked by level.
HEALTHYour current and maximum PSI alongside health and fatigue.
SKILLS PSIYour Psionics skill level and remaining uses.

The live syntax is PSI <ability> [AT] <target>. Leaving off the target attempts to use the power on yourself.

Starter abilities verified

Centering

PSI CNTR begins meditation that restores psionic energy. It ends when the effect finishes or your mind is refreshed. Centering itself is not a skill-use advancement action.

Dart

PSI DART <target> makes a mental attack. A target can resist or the effect can otherwise fail, so “Nothing happens” does not necessarily mean the command was wrong.

Telepathy

PSI ESP on yourself enables telepathic contact. While it is active, use CONTACT <player> <message>.

A safe practice loop

  1. Run INFO PSI and HEALTH.
  2. Choose an available ability and a suitable target. Get consent before targeting another player.
  3. Enter the power with PSI <short name> <target>.
  4. Check HEALTH for PSI and SKILLS PSI for progression.
  5. Use PSI CNTR when you need active recovery.

The live game is authoritative. PSI costs, regeneration, resistance, level unlocks, and effects remain under beta testing.

Current level-one display

The tested Human Psion showed CNTR (Centering), DART (Dart), and ESP (Telepathy) as available. Detect Psionics, Detect Invisible, and Examine Aura appeared as locked by level. This is a snapshot, not a promise that every build will receive the same list.
