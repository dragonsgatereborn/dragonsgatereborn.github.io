Movement and information

Use LOOK to read rooms and displayed exits; move with full or abbreviated directions. Use INFO, HEALTH, INVENTORY, and SKILLS to understand your character.

Equipment and shops

LIST shows a shop's stock. BUY <number> obtains an item. Use READY for a weapon and WEAR for armor or shields. Recheck inventory numbers after equipping.

Combat and positioning

KILL <target> is the verified basic melee attack. ASSESS reviews positions and RETREAT changes range within a room. Combat balance and class actions continue to be refined.

Reporting and assistance

Try HELP <command> first, while remembering that some help files remain incomplete. Use Discord for normal questions and testing discussion. Use ORACLE <message> when an issue genuinely requires staff assistance.
